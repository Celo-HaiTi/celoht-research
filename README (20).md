## What is CeloHT?

CeloHT (Celo-HT) is a Haitian-led, community-governed, open-source social-impact initiative focused on financial inclusion, blockchain education, and environmental sustainability in Haiti. It is built on the Celo blockchain ecosystem as an infrastructure choice — **CeloHT is not affiliated with Celo Foundation or Valora, is not a cryptocurrency, and is not an investment platform.**

**No Token Policy:** CeloHT has no native token, no ICO, no presale, no staking token, no speculation model, and makes no investment promises. It uses only existing Celo network assets — cUSD for payments, CELO for gas.

CeloHT is organized around three permanent pillars:

| Pillar | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 🎓 **Education** | Web3 and financial literacy education, delivered primarily in Haitian Creole |
| 🤝 **Agent Network** | Local, human support networks that help communities access digital financial tools |
| 🌱 **Reforestation** | Sustainable development and environmental impact tied to community growth |

Learn more: [celoht.com](https://celoht.com) · Documentation: [github.com/Celo-HT/CeloHT](https://github.com/Celo-HT/CeloHT)

## Why This Repository Exists

A strong visual identity builds trust — with the communities CeloHT serves, with contributors, with NGOs, universities, and potential partners. This repository is the **single source of truth** for how CeloHT looks, sounds, and presents itself anywhere in the world.

If you are building anything that carries the CeloHT name or mark — a slide deck, a t-shirt, a hackathon banner, a partner co-branded page — this repository tells you exactly how to do it correctly.

## Core Principles

CeloHT's identity is built on:

- **Open Source** — everything here is free to use under the terms in [LICENSE](./LICENSE) and [LOGO_GUIDELINES.md](./LOGO_GUIDELINES.md)
- **Transparency** — no hidden agendas, no dark patterns, no confusing disclaimers
- **Community First** — this repository belongs to the CeloHT community, not one individual
- **Financial Inclusion & Education** — the visual identity must feel accessible to first-time smartphone users, not just crypto-native audiences
- **Sustainability** — CeloHT's brand should age well and require minimal redesign over the years
- **Non-Affiliation** — CeloHT clearly and consistently discloses that Celo blockchain and Valora wallet compatibility is a technical choice, not an endorsement or partnership

## Non-Affiliation Disclaimer

CeloHT uses the Celo blockchain and supports the Valora wallet as **infrastructure choices only**. CeloHT is an independent, community-governed initiative and is **not officially affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Celo Foundation or Valora**. This disclaimer must appear on all official CeloHT materials that reference Celo or Valora — see [BRAND_GUIDE.md](./BRAND_GUIDE.md#non-affiliation--disclaimers) for exact required wording.
