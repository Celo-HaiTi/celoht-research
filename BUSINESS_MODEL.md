# Business Model

## An Important Clarification

CeloHT is **not an investment**. There's no equity, no promised return, and no financial instrument sold as a wealth-building opportunity. This is the operating model for a community-governed, nonprofit-style initiative — not a commercial company.

## Funding Sources

### 1. Ecosystem Grants
Applications to Celo Foundation grant programs and other funding aligned with financial inclusion and environmental sustainability.

### 2. Strategic Partnerships
Collaboration with organizations that fund or support specific projects — for example, a dedicated grant for a reforestation cycle. See [PARTNERS.md](./PARTNERS.md).

### 3. Community Contributions
Voluntary donations and support through channels like GitHub Sponsors (see `.github/FUNDING.yml`).

## How Resources Get Used

- Developing and translating educational material
- Operational support for the Agent Network (training, materials)
- cUSD rewards for verified reforestation activity
- Technical infrastructure — website, documentation, workflows

## Long-Term Sustainability

The long-term goal is a model where the Agent Network becomes increasingly self-sustaining through reasonable service fees, reducing dependence on grants alone over time — see [ROADMAP.md](./ROADMAP.md#phase-3--growth-2027) for when this begins, and [FUNDING_POLICY.md](./FUNDING_POLICY.md) for the full funding governance policy.

## Relationship to FreClean

FreClean, the founder's second initiative, is a cleaning services business operating separately but sharing a vision around local entrepreneurship. The long-term plan includes formalizing a partnership between the two initiatives, where FreClean's traction serves as proof of execution for CeloHT's funders. See [FOUNDER.md](./FOUNDER.md).

## What This Model Deliberately Avoids

No native token, no ICO, no presale, no staking product, no yield product, no equity offering — see [NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md](./NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md) for the full policy and the reasoning behind it.
