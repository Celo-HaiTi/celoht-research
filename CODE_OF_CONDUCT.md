# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as members, contributors, and maintainers of CeloHT, pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We are especially committed to being welcoming to first-time open-source contributors and to non-English speakers.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes: using welcoming and inclusive language; being respectful of differing viewpoints; gracefully accepting constructive criticism; focusing on what's best for the community; showing empathy toward other members; being patient with contributors who are new to open source or to blockchain concepts.

Unacceptable behavior includes: harassment or derogatory comments in any form; publishing others' private information without consent; trolling or sustained disruption; misrepresenting CeloHT as a token, investment, or speculative opportunity in any community space.

## Scope

Applies within all CeloHT spaces (GitHub, Discord, Telegram, social media, in-person events) and when representing CeloHT publicly.

## Enforcement

Report violations to **contact@celoht.com**. Reports are reviewed promptly and confidentially by community maintainers.

**Guidelines:** (1) Correction — private warning; (2) Warning — formal warning with consequences for repetition; (3) Temporary Ban; (4) Permanent Ban — for severe or repeated violations.
