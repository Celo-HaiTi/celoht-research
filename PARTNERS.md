# Partners

## Partnership Philosophy

CeloHT partners with organizations that share its commitment to transparency, community governance, and non-speculative financial inclusion. Partnerships are structured around specific pillar outcomes (Education, Agent Network, or Reforestation), not general sponsorship of the CeloHT brand.

## Partner Categories

| Category | Typical collaboration |
|---|---|
| NGOs | Train-the-trainer education delivery, community outreach, Agent Network regional support |
| Governments | Policy dialogue, regulatory alignment, public education initiatives |
| Universities | Curriculum co-development, research on financial inclusion outcomes, case study collaboration |
| Companies | Technical contribution, in-kind support, employee volunteer programs |
| Open-source communities | Code contribution, security review, tooling |
| Financial institutions | Cash-in/cash-out infrastructure collaboration with the Agent Network |
| Climate organizations | Reforestation methodology, monitoring, and species selection guidance |
| Developers | Direct code and documentation contribution |
| Sponsors / grant partners | Funding tied to specific pillar outcomes |
| Media | Coverage, press collaboration (see the Brand repository's `PRESS_KIT.md`) |
| Global / regional ambassadors | Community representation and outreach in a specific region |
| Integration / technical partners | Wallet, payment rail, or infrastructure integration |

## Partnership Terms

All partnerships are governed by a written agreement specifying: scope, funding (if any) and its earmarked pillar, duration, and co-branding terms per the Brand repository's `PARTNERSHIP_BRANDING.md`. No partnership grants exclusive rights to the CeloHT name, nor implies investment in or ownership of CeloHT.

## What CeloHT Does Not Offer Partners

- Equity, tokens, or any speculative financial instrument (see [NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md](./NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md))
- Exclusive territorial rights
- Control over CeloHT's governance in exchange for funding

## How to Propose a Partnership

1. Email contact@celoht.com with an overview of your organization and proposed collaboration
2. Initial review by the relevant Working Group (see [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md#working-groups))
3. For funding-based partnerships, terms are drafted and reviewed against the Funding Governance policy in [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md#funding-governance)
4. Agreement signed and (for material partnerships) publicly disclosed here

## Funding Transparency

Funding received and its pillar allocation is reported in aggregate in [METRICS.md](./METRICS.md) and, for individual major grants, disclosed here with the partner's consent.
