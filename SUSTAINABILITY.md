# Sustainability

## Two Meanings, One Standard

CeloHT uses "sustainability" in two connected senses, both held to the same standard of durability over hype:

1. **Environmental sustainability** — the Reforestation pillar (see [REFORESTATION.md](./REFORESTATION.md))
2. **Organizational sustainability** — CeloHT's ability to keep operating and delivering impact over years, not a single funding cycle

## Environmental Sustainability

Covered in full in [REFORESTATION.md](./REFORESTATION.md). Core principle: reforestation impact is measured by verified long-term survival, not planting events, and is community-owned so it continues independent of any single funding cycle.

## Organizational Sustainability

### Funding Diversification
CeloHT's funding model (see [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md#funding-governance) and [WHITEPAPER.md](./WHITEPAPER.md#6-economic-model--sustainability)) deliberately spans grants, partnerships, and transparent Agent Network fees rather than depending on a single funder — reducing the risk that losing one funding source halts the entire initiative.

### Governance Continuity
CeloHT's community governance structure (see [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md)) with staggered Maintainer Council terms and distributed Working Group leadership is designed so the project doesn't depend on any single individual, including the founder, to continue functioning.

### Knowledge Continuity
This documentation repository itself is a sustainability mechanism — comprehensive, versioned documentation (see [CHANGELOG.md](./CHANGELOG.md)) ensures institutional knowledge survives contributor and maintainer turnover, a common failure point for open-source and NGO projects alike.

### Technical Sustainability
- Open-source code (no vendor lock-in)
- Minimal on-chain footprint, keeping operational costs predictable (see [SMART_CONTRACTS.md](./SMART_CONTRACTS.md#design-principles))
- Standard, well-supported tooling (Solidity/Hardhat, Next.js) to keep the contributor pool as wide as possible

### Community Sustainability
Agent Network compensation (see [AGENT_NETWORK.md](./AGENT_NETWORK.md#revenue-model)) and community nursery incentives (see [REFORESTATION.md](./REFORESTATION.md#community-incentives)) are structured to be sustainable, published, and fair — not dependent on volunteer goodwill alone for core operational roles.

## What Sustainability Is Not, at CeloHT

Sustainability at CeloHT never means offsetting environmental or social cost through a token or speculative financial mechanism — see [NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md](./NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md). Sustainability and speculation are treated as opposites here, not complements.
