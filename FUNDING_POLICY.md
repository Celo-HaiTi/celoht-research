# Funding Policy

## Accepted Funding Types

- Grants from foundations, NGOs, and impact-focused funders
- Partnership funding earmarked to a specific pillar or initiative
- In-kind contributions (technical, educational, logistical)
- Diaspora and community donations
- Agent Network transaction fees (operational revenue, not external funding, but governed by the same transparency standard — see [AGENT_NETWORK.md](./AGENT_NETWORK.md#revenue-model))

## Never Accepted

- Token-based or equity-based funding structures of any kind — see [NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md](./NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md)
- Funding conditioned on suppressing negative metrics or impact findings (see [METRICS.md](./METRICS.md#corrections-policy))
- Funding that would require CeloHT to promote a funder's product as an investment or in a way inconsistent with [VOICE_AND_TONE.md](https://github.com/Celo-HT/Brand/blob/main/VOICE_AND_TONE.md) (Brand repository)
- Anonymous funding above a threshold set by the Governance & Legal Working Group, to maintain funding-source transparency

## Allocation Process

1. Funding is earmarked to a pillar or specific initiative at the time of agreement (see [PARTNERS.md](./PARTNERS.md#partnership-terms))
2. The relevant Working Group manages utilization against the earmark
3. Utilization is reported quarterly against the original earmark, with variance explained if actual use diverges from plan

## Transparency Reporting

Aggregate funding sources and pillar allocation are published in [METRICS.md](./METRICS.md) and CeloHT's quarterly/annual impact reports (see [METRICS.md](./METRICS.md#reporting-cadence)). Individual major grants are disclosed in [PARTNERS.md](./PARTNERS.md) with funder consent.

## Reserve Policy

CeloHT's Governance & Legal Working Group maintains a minimum operating reserve target (published internally, summarized in the annual report) to ensure continuity of core operations (Agent Network compliance support, education platform hosting) through funding gaps.

## Conflicts of Interest

Maintainer Council or Working Group members with a financial relationship to a prospective funder must disclose it and recuse themselves from the funding decision, per [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md#ethics).
