# Blog Guide

## Purpose

Standards for content published on CeloHT's Medium/blog channel — technical writeups, impact reports, and mission-driven storytelling.

## Content Categories

| Category | Cadence | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Impact reports | Quarterly, matching [METRICS.md](./METRICS.md#reporting-cadence) | "Q2 2026: 4,200 people trained, 47 active agents" |
| Technical deep-dives | As shipped | "How CeloHT's AgentRegistry works" |
| Governance updates | As decided | "RFC-014: Approved — updated Agent Network fee schedule" |
| Community/success stories | Ongoing | See [SUCCESS_STORIES.md](./SUCCESS_STORIES.md) |
| Educational explainers | Ongoing | "What is cUSD, really?" (public-friendly version of [CUSD.md](./CUSD.md)) |

## Voice & Style

Follows the Brand repository's `VOICE_AND_TONE.md` — clear, warm, confident without hype. Technical posts may go deeper than general brand voice guidelines allow, but must remain accessible to a reader without a blockchain background in the opening paragraphs, with technical depth building progressively.

## Required Elements

- Every post mentioning cUSD, CELO, or CeloHT's model must be consistent with [NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md](./NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md) — no language implying investment or speculative opportunity
- Every impact figure cited must match the current published figures in [METRICS.md](./METRICS.md) — no rounding up or cherry-picking favorable timeframes
- Technical posts referencing code should link to the actual repository/file, not just describe it

## Review Process

Impact and governance posts are reviewed by the relevant Working Group before publication (see [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md#working-groups)); technical posts are reviewed by the Engineering Working Group for accuracy.

## SEO & Accessibility

- Descriptive, honest headlines — no clickbait framing, especially avoiding financial-return-implying language
- Alt text on all images
- Headings structured hierarchically (not skipped levels) for screen-reader navigation
