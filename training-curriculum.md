# Agent Operations Manual: Training Curriculum

*Detailed operational companion to [../AGENT_NETWORK.md](../AGENT_NETWORK.md#training-curriculum) and [onboarding-and-verification.md](./onboarding-and-verification.md).*

## Training Structure

Delivered over 2–3 days (in-person, regional cohort-based) by a Regional Coordinator or certified Education Working Group trainer, followed by a supervised probation period before full activation.

## Day 1: Mission, Policy, and Product

### Session 1.1 — CeloHT Mission & Values
Agents must be able to explain CeloHT's three pillars and, critically, correctly and confidently explain the No-Token Policy to skeptical or confused users — see [../NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md](../NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md). This is treated as core competency, not background context, because agents are frequently a new user's very first point of contact with CeloHT.

### Session 1.2 — Wallet Operations
Hands-on: Valora setup, transaction verification, reading a transaction confirmation screen — mirrors [education module 4](../education/module-04-wallet-safety.md) but at agent-operational depth (e.g. handling a user's setup issues, not just their own).

### Session 1.3 — cUSD/CELO Deep Dive
Beyond user-level understanding: float management concepts, transaction fee estimation, and how gas costs (in CELO) factor into agent operations — see [../CUSD.md](../CUSD.md) and [../CELO.md](../CELO.md).

## Day 2: Operations & Compliance

### Session 2.1 — Cash Handling Procedures
Physical cash security practices, daily reconciliation process (matching physical cash + digital float against transaction logs), and end-of-day reporting.

### Session 2.2 — Compliance & KYC
Practical training on the KYC practices described in [../AGENT_NETWORK.md](../AGENT_NETWORK.md#compliance) — how to apply verification proportionate to transaction size, and documentation requirements.

### Session 2.3 — Fraud Recognition
Pattern-based training covering: suspicious transaction velocity, coached/pressured users (a red flag for external scam involvement), and fake "CeloHT" impersonation attempts targeting agents themselves. See [risk-management.md](./risk-management.md).

## Day 3: Field Practice & Assessment

### Session 3.1 — Customer Service & De-escalation
Role-play scenarios: a frustrated user, a mismatched transaction confirmation, a user asking the agent to bypass verification "just this once" (correct answer: never).

### Session 3.2 — Supervised Practice Transactions
Real (testnet) transactions conducted under Regional Coordinator supervision.

### Session 3.3 — Certification Assessment
Per [onboarding-and-verification.md](./onboarding-and-verification.md#step-6-certification-assessment).

## Recertification Training (Annual)

Abbreviated half-day session covering: policy updates, newly identified fraud patterns since last certification, and a refresher practical assessment.

## Trainer Requirements

Regional Coordinators must themselves be certified agents with at least 6 months of active, compliant operation before becoming eligible to deliver training — ensuring trainers teach from real operational experience, not only the written manual.
