# Legal Status

> This document provides general information about CeloHT's structure and disclaimers. It is not legal advice. Consult a qualified attorney for jurisdiction-specific guidance.

## Organizational Structure

CeloHT operates as a community-governed, open-source initiative. Its governance structure is documented in [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md). CeloHT's specific legal entity structure (e.g. nonprofit registration status, if applicable in a given jurisdiction) is disclosed at [celoht.com/legal](https://celoht.com) and updated as it evolves.

## Non-Affiliation Disclaimer

CeloHT uses the Celo blockchain and supports the Valora wallet as an infrastructure choice. **CeloHT is an independent, community-governed initiative and is not officially affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Celo Foundation or Valora.**

## No Token / No Investment Status

CeloHT has no native token, issues no securities, and offers no investment product. See the full policy at [NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md](./NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md). This status is a core part of CeloHT's legal risk posture — by design, CeloHT does not engage in activity that would typically trigger securities regulation applicable to token issuance or investment solicitation.

## Regulatory Posture

CeloHT's Agent Network operates under transaction limits and KYC practices designed to align with applicable local financial services regulation in each region of operation — see [AGENT_NETWORK.md](./AGENT_NETWORK.md#compliance). CeloHT monitors regulatory developments affecting digital payments and financial inclusion in its operating regions and adjusts Agent Network compliance practices accordingly, through the Governance & Legal Working Group (see [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md#working-groups)).

## Privacy

CeloHT collects the minimum data necessary to operate its education platform, agent verification system, and impact reporting. Personally identifiable information is never stored on-chain (see [SMART_CONTRACTS.md](./SMART_CONTRACTS.md#design-principles)). A full privacy policy is published and maintained at [celoht.com/privacy](https://celoht.com).
