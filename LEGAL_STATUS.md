# CeloHT Legal Status

**Version 1.0 · August 2026**

This document describes the legal nature, governance philosophy, and operating principles of CeloHT. It is published for the benefit of contributors, community members, grant reviewers, donors, ecosystem partners, and any institution evaluating CeloHT for collaboration, funding, or integration.

This document is informational. It is not a substitute for independent legal advice, and no statement in this document should be relied upon as a legal opinion regarding CeloHT's status in any specific jurisdiction. Where CeloHT's legal form evolves — for example, through future incorporation — this document will be updated, versioned, and the change will be publicly disclosed (see Section 22).

## 1. Purpose

This document exists to state, plainly and accurately, what CeloHT is, how it operates, and what legal claims it does and does not make about itself. Open-source, community-driven projects increasingly interact with institutional actors — grant programs, universities, foundations, ecosystem partners, and government-adjacent bodies — that require clarity about a project's legal nature before engaging. CeloHT publishes this document so that any such actor can evaluate the project on accurate terms, without ambiguity or overstatement.

This document is intended to be read alongside `GOVERNANCE.md` (which describes CeloHT's internal decision-making structure) and `ARCHITECTURE.md` (which describes CeloHT's technical systems). Together, these three documents give a complete picture of how CeloHT is organized, governed, and built.

---

## 2. Mission

CeloHT is an open-source, community-governed initiative built on the Celo blockchain, organized around three coordinated areas of work:

- **Financial Inclusion and Web3 Education** — delivering financial literacy and blockchain education in Haitian Creole and other languages, so that participation in digital financial tools is informed and accessible.
- **Community Agent Network** — supporting a network of community-based agents who facilitate access to stable-value digital payments, primarily using the cUSD stablecoin on the Celo network.
- **Environmental Restoration (Reforestation)** — coordinating and transparently reporting on tree-planting and environmental-restoration activity carried out with and by participating communities.

CeloHT pursues this mission as a public-benefit-oriented, non-commercial initiative. It does not pursue this mission through the issuance of any token, security, or investment product.

## 3. Legal Position

CeloHT is, at present, an open-source community initiative. It has not been formally incorporated, registered, or recognized as a nonprofit, foundation, charity, company, or any other legal entity in any jurisdiction, unless and until a specific, dated public notice states otherwise on this page.

Accordingly, as of the date of this document:

- CeloHT does **not** hold nonprofit or charitable registration in any jurisdiction.
- CeloHT does **not** hold tax-exempt status of any kind.
- CeloHT is **not** incorporated as a company, foundation, association, or any other legal entity.
- CeloHT has **not** received, and does not claim to have received, approval, endorsement, license, or authorization from any government or regulatory authority.
- Individuals contributing to CeloHT do so as members of an open-source community, not as employees, officers, or legal representatives of a registered organization, unless a separate, explicitly disclosed agreement states otherwise.

This is a factual description of CeloHT's current organizational form, not a limitation the project considers permanent. Section 20 describes how this may change over time, and how any change will be communicated.

## 4. Nature of the Project

To avoid ambiguity, CeloHT states explicitly what it is and is not.

### 4.1 What CeloHT Is

CeloHT is:

- An open-source software project, with publicly available source code, issue trackers, and contribution history.
- A community education initiative, producing openly published educational material.
- A coordinating body for a volunteer and community-based Agent Network engaged in facilitating access to stable-value digital payments.
- A coordinator and public reporter of community reforestation activity.
- A community governed through documented, published governance processes (see `GOVERNANCE.md`).

### 4.2 What CeloHT Is Not

CeloHT is explicitly **not**:

- **A bank or licensed financial institution.** CeloHT does not hold customer funds in a banking capacity, does not offer deposit accounts, and does not provide banking services.
- **A cryptocurrency exchange.** CeloHT does not operate a trading platform, order book, or asset-exchange service.
- **An investment company or investment adviser.** CeloHT does not manage investments on behalf of others and does not offer investment advice.
- **A security issuer.** CeloHT has not issued, and does not intend to issue, any instrument that could reasonably be characterized as a security under applicable law.
- **A token issuer.** CeloHT has no native token, has never conducted a token sale, and does not plan to create one. References to cUSD and CELO in CeloHT materials refer exclusively to existing, independently issued Celo-network assets used as payment and network infrastructure — not to any CeloHT-created instrument.
- **A DAO with legal personality.** CeloHT does not operate on-chain token-based voting and does not claim any form of legal personality arising from decentralized governance structures.
- **A for-profit company.** CeloHT does not distribute profits to any individual and does not operate with a profit-maximization objective.

## 5. Public Benefit Commitment

CeloHT commits to directing its activities toward outcomes that benefit the communities it serves, rather than toward the private financial benefit of any founder, contributor, or affiliated individual. This commitment is expressed structurally, not merely as a stated intention:

- Governance authority is distributed across a Governance Council and Working Groups, not concentrated in a single individual (see `GOVERNANCE.md`).
- Treasury funds are earmarked for programmatic and operational use, with disbursement rules and public reporting described in Sections 8 and 9 below.
- Educational materials, software, and documentation are published under open licenses (Section 11), so that the value CeloHT creates remains available to the public rather than becoming proprietary.

CeloHT does not use the language of "public benefit" as a marketing term; it uses the term to describe a set of enforceable internal rules, documented in `GOVERNANCE.md`, that structurally constrain how the project's resources and authority may be used.

## 6. Governance Principles

CeloHT is governed according to a published governance framework, `GOVERNANCE.md`, which establishes:

- A **Governance Council** responsible for strategic and treasury decisions, elected on a defined term and subject to recall.
- **Working Groups** with defined charters covering Education, Technology, Community, Agent Network, Reforestation, Finance, Communications, and Legal matters.
- A **Proposal Lifecycle** requiring public discussion, a comment period, and a recorded vote before strategic or treasury decisions take effect.
- Defined **voting thresholds** (Simple Majority, Absolute Majority, Two-Thirds Majority, Super Majority) scaled to the significance of the decision being made.

This document does not restate the full governance framework; it affirms that CeloHT's legal-position statements in this document are enforced through that governance structure, and that any change to CeloHT's legal form (Section 20) must proceed through the same governance and amendment process.

## 7. Transparency Commitments

CeloHT publishes, on an ongoing basis and through public, version-controlled channels:

- Governance documents and amendments (`GOVERNANCE.md` and its changelog).
- Meeting notes from Governance Council and Working Group sessions.
- Quarterly and annual financial reports (Section 8).
- A public impact dashboard reporting education, Agent Network, and reforestation metrics.
- Release notes for all software releases.

Where information is temporarily withheld — for example, during an active security investigation — CeloHT commits to disclosing that information is being withheld, and why, rather than remaining silent without explanation.

## 8. Financial Accountability

CeloHT commits to handling all funds it receives — whether grants, donations, or programmatic revenue — with documented accountability procedures:

- **Budget approval** for programmatic and operational spending follows a defined internal approval process described in `GOVERNANCE.md`.
- **Quarterly reports** summarizing income, expenses by category, and treasury balance are published within 30 days of each quarter's end.
- **Annual reports** summarizing full-year financial activity, program outcomes, and governance activity are published within 60 days of year-end.
- **Independent financial review.** Once annual treasury flow exceeds a defined threshold, CeloHT commits to an annual independent financial review conducted by a qualified third party, with results published in summary form.

CeloHT does not currently issue tax receipts of any kind, because it does not hold tax-exempt or charitable registration in any jurisdiction (Section 3). Donors and grant-makers should evaluate the tax treatment of any contribution to CeloHT with their own advisors, based on their jurisdiction and CeloHT's actual legal status at the time of the contribution.

## 9. Treasury Principles

- Funds held in CeloHT's name are used exclusively for programmatic and operational purposes aligned with the mission described in Section 2 — never for personal enrichment of any founder, contributor, Council member, or affiliated individual.
- No individual, including the project's founder, may unilaterally authorize a treasury disbursement above the operational thresholds defined in `GOVERNANCE.md`; disbursements above those thresholds require multi-person or Council-level approval.
- CeloHT does not use treasury funds to purchase volatile or speculative assets for trading purposes. Stablecoin holdings (cUSD) are used as an operational settlement currency, not as an investment position.
- CeloHT maintains an operational reserve intended to sustain core activity during funding gaps, sized and governed according to the principles in `GOVERNANCE.md`.

These are internal governance commitments, not the equivalent of a legally mandated fiduciary duty that would apply to a registered nonprofit or company. Institutions requiring the latter should refer to Section 20 regarding CeloHT's future institutional development plans and should evaluate any specific transaction accordingly.

## 10. Intellectual Property

- Software, documentation, and educational materials created for CeloHT are developed collaboratively by contributors and published in public repositories.
- Contributors retain their moral rights as authors under applicable law, while granting the community the rights described in the relevant open-source license (Section 11) to use, modify, and redistribute their contributions.
- CeloHT's name, logo, and visual identity are used to identify the project and its official channels; use of CeloHT branding in a way that suggests unauthorized endorsement, partnership, or affiliation is not permitted.
- Because CeloHT is not currently a registered legal entity, formal trademark registration is not in place as of the date of this document; CeloHT relies on consistent public use and documentation to establish its identity and reserves the right to pursue formal protection in the future (Section 20).

## 11. Open Source Licensing

CeloHT publishes its software and documentation under open-source licenses selected on a per-repository basis and stated explicitly in each repository's `LICENSE` file. CeloHT's approach to licensing follows widely recognized open-source norms:

- Source code is publicly accessible in CeloHT's GitHub organization.
- License terms are stated clearly at the repository level, so that any user, contributor, or integrating partner can determine their rights and obligations without ambiguity.
- CeloHT does not impose licensing terms that restrict use in a way inconsistent with recognized open-source definitions (such as those maintained by the Open Source Initiative), except where a specific repository explicitly states otherwise for a documented reason.
- Contributions are accepted under the license terms stated in the relevant repository's contribution guidelines at the time of contribution.

Any partner or institution planning to build on, redistribute, or integrate CeloHT software should consult the specific `LICENSE` file of the repository in question, as terms may vary by repository and may be updated over time.

## 12. Community Responsibilities

Participation in CeloHT — as a contributor, maintainer, Working Group member, Agent Network participant, or community member — carries the following expectations:

- Adherence to CeloHT's published Code of Conduct in all official spaces.
- Honest representation of one's role and authority when engaging with partners, donors, or the public on CeloHT's behalf.
- Disclosure of any conflict of interest relevant to a decision, consistent with Section 15.
- Compliance with applicable law in one's own jurisdiction when participating in CeloHT activities, particularly Agent Network activity involving the movement of funds.

CeloHT provides governance structures (`GOVERNANCE.md`) and this Legal Status document to support community members in meeting these expectations, but ultimate legal compliance in each individual's home jurisdiction remains that individual's own responsibility.

## 13. Compliance Principles

CeloHT is committed to conducting its activities in a manner consistent with applicable law in the jurisdictions where it operates, recognizing that as an early-stage, non-incorporated community initiative, its compliance obligations differ from those of a registered entity. CeloHT's compliance approach includes:

- Ongoing monitoring of regulatory developments relevant to community-based payment facilitation (Agent Network activity) and blockchain-based tools, coordinated by the Legal Working Group described in `GOVERNANCE.md`.
- A structural preference for activity that reduces regulatory ambiguity — for example, using an existing, independently issued stablecoin (cUSD) rather than issuing a CeloHT-specific asset.
- A commitment to pause or restructure any specific activity found, on legal review, to require licensing or registration CeloHT does not hold, rather than continuing that activity unlicensed.
- Willingness to engage constructively with regulators, grant-making institutions, and legal counsel as the project matures and its institutional form evolves (Section 20).

This document does not constitute, and should not be relied upon as, a compliance opinion for any specific jurisdiction. Partners operating in a specific regulatory environment should conduct their own legal review before engaging in activity that depends on CeloHT's regulatory status.

## 14. Anti-Corruption Statement

CeloHT prohibits, without exception, the offering, giving, solicitation, or acceptance of any bribe, kickback, or improper payment intended to influence a governance decision, grant award, vendor selection, Agent Network appointment, or any other CeloHT decision.

- Any individual acting in an official CeloHT capacity who becomes aware of actual or suspected corrupt activity is expected to report it to the Governance Council, or, where a Council member is implicated, to the remaining unconflicted Council members.
- Confirmed violations are grounds for immediate removal from any CeloHT role and, where funds are involved, referral to appropriate authorities in the relevant jurisdiction.
- This statement applies equally to the Founder, Governance Council members, Maintainers, Working Group leads, Ambassadors, and any paid contractor or vendor engaged by CeloHT.

---

## 15. Conflict of Interest Principles

CeloHT requires disclosure of any situation in which an individual in a position of authority — a Council member, Maintainer, or Working Group lead — or a close family member, stands to personally benefit from a decision under consideration.

- Disclosed conflicts are logged and reviewed under the process described in `GOVERNANCE.md`.
- An individual with a disclosed conflict of interest may contribute relevant information to a discussion but may not vote on the matter and is not counted toward quorum for that vote.
- This principle applies to treasury decisions, vendor and partnership selection, and role appointments alike.

---

## 16. Data Protection Commitment

CeloHT collects personal data only where necessary to operate its Education, Agent Network, and Reforestation programs — for example, learner progress records, agent verification information, and reforestation-event participant records.

- Personal data is stored in CeloHT's off-chain systems, not published on the public blockchain, and not included in public dashboard reporting in identifying form.
- Access to personal data is restricted on a role-appropriate basis (see `ARCHITECTURE.md`, Section 12, on access control).
- CeloHT does not sell personal data to third parties and does not use personal data for purposes unrelated to its stated mission without the affected individual's knowledge.
- Because CeloHT is not currently a registered legal entity, it does not hold formal data-protection-authority registration in any jurisdiction. CeloHT nonetheless commits to data-handling practices consistent with widely recognized data-minimization and purpose-limitation principles, and will formalize compliance obligations applicable under specific data-protection regimes (such as the EU's GDPR or comparable regional laws) as its institutional form and operational footprint develop (Section 20).

## 17. Risk Management

CeloHT recognizes the following categories of risk inherent to an early-stage, non-incorporated, community-governed initiative operating with blockchain-based tools in emerging-market contexts, and manages them through the structures described in `GOVERNANCE.md`:

| Risk category | Nature of risk | Mitigating structure |
|---|---|---|
| **Legal / regulatory** | Uncertainty regarding the legal characterization of Agent Network activity or future institutional form | Legal Working Group monitoring, compliance-first design choices (Section 13), phased institutional development (Section 20) |
| **Financial** | Treasury mismanagement, donor concentration, budget shortfall | Multi-person approval thresholds, quarterly reporting, operational reserve (Sections 8–9) |
| **Governance** | Concentration of authority, decision-making paralysis | Distributed Council structure, defined voting thresholds, term limits (`GOVERNANCE.md`) |
| **Operational** | Key-person dependency, Working Group inactivity | Documented processes, cross-training, defined role-review cycles |
| **Reputational** | Public confusion about CeloHT's legal or financial nature | This document and its ongoing accurate publication |

## 18. Partnership Principles

CeloHT welcomes collaboration with NGOs, universities, foundations, grant programs, ecosystem partners, and government-adjacent bodies, subject to the following principles:

- Partnerships must be consistent with CeloHT's mission (Section 2) and must not require CeloHT to misrepresent its legal status.
- Formal partnerships are documented through a Memorandum of Understanding or comparable written agreement, reviewed by the Legal Working Group prior to execution.
- CeloHT will disclose its current legal status (Section 3) to any prospective partner or funder as part of due diligence, rather than allowing a partner to assume a legal status CeloHT does not hold.
- Where a partner's requirements (for example, a grant program requiring disbursement to a registered legal entity) exceed CeloHT's current institutional capacity, CeloHT will state this directly rather than attempt to work around it through misrepresentation.

## 19. Independence Statement

CeloHT operates as an independent, community-governed initiative. It is not owned or controlled by any single individual, company, government, or external organization.

- The Celo blockchain is used as underlying technical infrastructure; this use does not constitute affiliation with, endorsement by, or control by the Celo Foundation or any other entity in the Celo ecosystem, unless a specific, separately disclosed partnership agreement states otherwise.
- CeloHT's Founder holds a defined, accountable role within the governance structure described in `GOVERNANCE.md`, not unilateral control over the project's direction, treasury, or public statements.
- Any funding, grant, or partnership relationship CeloHT enters into is structured to preserve CeloHT's governance independence; CeloHT does not accept funding conditioned on ceding governance authority described in `GOVERNANCE.md` to an external party.

## 20. Future Institutional Development

CeloHT's current non-incorporated status reflects its early stage of development, not a permanent design choice. As the project matures, the Governance Council may evaluate options for formal institutional development, which could include:

- Incorporation as a nonprofit, foundation, or association in one or more jurisdictions.
- Application for tax-exempt or charitable status where applicable and appropriate.
- Formal trademark registration for CeloHT's name and visual identity.
- Engagement of external legal counsel to formalize compliance programs referenced in Sections 13 and 16.

Any such development will:

- Be proposed and approved through the Strategic Decision process defined in `GOVERNANCE.md`, including a public comment period.
- Be publicly disclosed, with this document updated accordingly and the update reflected in the changelog described in Section 22.
- Not be announced or implied before it has actually occurred. CeloHT will not describe incorporation, registration, or tax-exempt status as "in progress," "pending," or "upcoming" in a way that could reasonably be mistaken for a current status, and will use dated, factual language for any such announcement when it occurs.

## 21. International Collaboration

CeloHT's mission is rooted in Haiti and the wider Caribbean but is designed to be technically and educationally extensible to other communities facing similar financial-inclusion and environmental challenges.

- CeloHT welcomes contributions, translations, and adaptations of its educational and technical materials by communities and institutions in other jurisdictions, subject to the licensing terms described in Section 11.
- Cross-border collaboration is conducted with awareness that legal, regulatory, and data-protection requirements vary by jurisdiction; CeloHT does not assume that principles suitable for one jurisdiction automatically satisfy the requirements of another, and encourages partners in new jurisdictions to conduct their own local legal review.
- CeloHT's Legal Working Group is responsible for flagging jurisdiction-specific issues as international collaboration expands.
