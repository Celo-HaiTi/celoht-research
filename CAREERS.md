# Careers & Paid Roles

## CeloHT's Employment Model

CeloHT is primarily a volunteer-driven, community-governed open-source initiative. Most participation — Working Group membership, code contribution, ambassador work — is voluntary, recognized through [AUTHORS.md](./AUTHORS.md) and community standing rather than salaried employment.

That said, certain roles are compensated, funded through the mechanisms described in [FUNDING_POLICY.md](./FUNDING_POLICY.md):

## Compensated Roles

| Role | Compensation basis |
|---|---|
| Agent Network members | Transparent per-transaction fee, paid in cUSD — see [AGENT_NETWORK.md](./AGENT_NETWORK.md#revenue-model) |
| Regional Coordinators | Stipend funded through earmarked grants, published per region |
| Reforestation nursery/monitoring staff | Transparent compensation per [REFORESTATION.md](./REFORESTATION.md#community-incentives) |
| Core Engineering/Education staff (where grant-funded) | Standard employment or contractor terms, disclosed in aggregate per [FUNDING_POLICY.md](./FUNDING_POLICY.md#transparency-reporting) |

**No role at CeloHT is compensated through a token, equity, or speculative mechanism** — see [NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md](./NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md).

## Open Positions

Current openings (grant-funded core roles, if any) are posted at [celoht.com/careers](https://celoht.com) and cross-posted to relevant Working Group channels. This document defines CeloHT's employment principles; specific live openings are maintained on the website since they change more frequently than this documentation review cycle.

## How to Get Involved Without a Paid Role

Most ways to contribute meaningfully to CeloHT don't require a compensated position — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md), [COMMUNITY.md](./COMMUNITY.md), and [WORKING_GROUPS.md](./WORKING_GROUPS.md#joining-a-working-group). Sustained volunteer contribution is also the most common path *into* an eventual compensated role, where one exists.

## Equal Opportunity

CeloHT is committed to fair, non-discriminatory practices in any compensated role, consistent with the inclusive standards in [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
