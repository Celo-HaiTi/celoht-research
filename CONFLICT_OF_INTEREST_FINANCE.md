# CeloHT Conflict of Interest Policy (Financial Matters)

**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** August 2026
**Status:** Active

---

## Purpose

This document defines how CeloHT identifies, discloses, and manages conflicts of interest specifically in financial decision-making — treasury disbursements, procurement, grant acceptance, and donor relationships. It applies and extends the general Conflict of Interest Policy in `GOVERNANCE.md` Section 10 and `LEGAL_STATUS.md` Section 15 to financial matters specifically.

## Scope

This policy applies to every individual with financial decision-making authority under `GOVERNANCE.md` and `TREASURY.md`: the Founder, Governance Council members, Treasury Committee members, Working Group Leads with budget responsibility, and any individual participating in a procurement or donation-review decision.

## Responsibilities

| Role | Financial COI Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Every financial decision-maker | Discloses relevant conflicts before participating in a decision |
| Governance Council | Reviews disclosures, resolves disputed recusal questions |
| Finance Working Group | Maintains the confidential disclosure register |

## Review Schedule

Reviewed annually per `GOVERNANCE.md` Section 21, alongside the Annual Declaration process in Section 4 below.

---

## 1. Disclosure Requirements

Any individual involved in a financial decision must disclose, before that decision is made, any situation in which they, a close family member, or an organization they materially benefit from stands to gain from the outcome. This includes:

- A vendor or procurement decision involving an entity the individual is affiliated with (`PROCUREMENT_POLICY.md` Section 3).
- A donation or grant from a source the individual has a personal or professional relationship with (`DONATION_POLICY.md`).
- Any treasury disbursement, including reimbursements, that would benefit the individual directly.

Disclosures are made in writing to the Governance Council and logged in a confidential register, with a summary reflected in public meeting notes where relevant to a specific recusal, consistent with `GOVERNANCE.md` Section 10.1.

## 2. Recusal Procedures

An individual with a disclosed financial conflict of interest:

- May participate in discussion to provide relevant factual information, if invited to do so by the deciding body.
- Must not vote on, or otherwise exercise approval authority over, the matter.
- Must not be counted toward quorum for that specific decision.
- Has their recusal explicitly logged in the decision record required by `INTERNAL_CONTROLS.md`.

## 3. Related-Party Transactions

A related-party transaction is any procurement, disbursement, reimbursement, or donation involving an individual with financial decision-making authority (or their close family member or affiliated organization) as counterparty.

- All related-party transactions require approval from unconflicted decision-makers only, regardless of the amount involved.
- Related-party transactions above the standard documentation threshold in `INTERNAL_CONTROLS.md` are flagged explicitly in the relevant Financial Report as a related-party transaction, with the nature of the relationship described in general terms sufficient for transparency without unnecessary personal detail.
- CeloHT does not enter into a related-party transaction where the terms are less favorable to CeloHT than a comparable arm's-length transaction would be.

## 4. Annual Declarations

Each individual holding a Governance Council, Treasury Committee, or budget-responsible Working Group Lead role completes an annual written declaration confirming:

- Any outside affiliations (employment, board membership, ownership interest) relevant to CeloHT's financial activity.
- Any close family relationship with another CeloHT role holder, vendor, or donor.
- Confirmation that all known conflicts during the prior year were disclosed at the time per Section 1.

Annual declarations are reviewed by the Governance Council and retained per the record-retention rules in `INTERNAL_CONTROLS.md`. A role holder who fails to submit an annual declaration is treated as non-compliant and subject to review under `GOVERNANCE.md` Section 3.9.
