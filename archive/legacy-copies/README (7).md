# Policy Research

**Summary:** Regulatory and policy analysis relevant to CeloHT's operations and expansion plans.
**Purpose:** Keep CeloHT's compliance posture current and informed as it grows across regions.
**Scope:** Digital payments regulation, financial-inclusion policy, blockchain-specific regulatory developments in Haiti and prospective expansion markets.
**Audience:** Governance & Legal, Maintainer Council, researchers.

## Overview

CeloHT's Agent Network operates under compliance practices designed to align with applicable regulation (see the main documentation repository's `AGENT_NETWORK.md#compliance` and `LEGAL_STATUS.md`). This area tracks the regulatory environment itself, so those practices stay current rather than static.

## Research Questions

1. How is Haiti's regulatory approach to digital payments and blockchain-based services evolving?
2. What regulatory considerations apply to the Caribbean expansion markets under consideration for later roadmap phases?
3. How do comparable financial-inclusion programs elsewhere navigate similar regulatory environments?

## Objectives

Provide the Governance & Legal side of the Maintainer Council with current, well-sourced regulatory analysis ahead of major decisions.

## Current Knowledge

Foundational stage.

## Data Sources

Published regulatory guidance, legal analysis from partner organizations, comparative policy research.

## Methodology

Policy and legal-document analysis — see [../../METHODOLOGY.md](../../METHODOLOGY.md).

## Limitations

Regulatory environments change; policy findings are dated explicitly and reviewed periodically rather than treated as permanent.

## Future Work

Comparative regulatory landscape review for candidate Phase 4+ expansion markets.
