# Governance Research

**Summary:** Research on CeloHT's community governance model and its effectiveness compared to alternatives.
**Purpose:** Test whether CeloHT's Foundation Director → Maintainer Council → Community Contributors model actually functions as intended.
**Scope:** Decision-making effectiveness, community participation, comparative governance-model research.
**Audience:** Maintainer Council, researchers, governance-focused partners.

## Overview

CeloHT deliberately chose participation-based governance over token-weighted voting (see the main documentation repository's `GOVERNANCE.md` and `NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md`). This area studies whether that structure delivers on its stated goals — broad participation, resistance to power concentration — as CeloHT grows.

## Research Questions

1. Who actually participates in CeloHT's governance process, and who's underrepresented?
2. How does CeloHT's model compare to token-weighted and pure-founder-led alternatives on decision quality and community trust?
3. Is the planned Phase 3 elected community input process (see the main documentation repository's `ROADMAP.md#phase-3--growth-2027`) achieving broader legitimacy than the current model?

## Objectives

Give the Maintainer Council evidence to refine governance as CeloHT scales, rather than assuming the current design will keep working unchanged.

## Current Knowledge

Foundational stage.

## Data Sources

Governance participation records (RFC engagement, PR review distribution), planned community governance-trust survey.

## Methodology

Mixed methods — participation data analysis plus qualitative interviews. See [../../METHODOLOGY.md](../../METHODOLOGY.md).

## Limitations

Small early-stage contributor base limits statistical power; early findings are descriptive rather than strongly inferential.

## Future Work

Comparative governance-model literature review ahead of the Phase 3 elected-input-process design.
