## How Prioritization Works

Items move up the roadmap based on active community need — e.g. if a partner needs co-branding guidelines for an upcoming NGO collaboration, `PARTNERSHIP_BRANDING.md` gets prioritized. Open an issue to request re-prioritization.

## Guiding Constraint

Every roadmap item ships as a **complete, production-ready document** — nothing is merged as a stub or placeholder. This keeps the repository trustworthy for anyone who depends on it, at the cost of shipping fewer items faster.
