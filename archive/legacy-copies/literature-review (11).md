# Literature Review: Financial Inclusion

**Summary:** External research relevant to CeloHT's financial-inclusion work, reviewed and summarized.
**Purpose:** Ground CeloHT's own research in existing knowledge rather than starting from zero.
**Scope:** Financial inclusion research relevant to Haiti and comparable low-connectivity, cash-dominant economies.
**Audience:** Researchers designing CeloHT studies.

## Scope of This Review

This review will cover: formal banking access research in Haiti, mobile money adoption studies from comparable Caribbean and Sub-Saharan African markets, and stablecoin-specific financial-inclusion research. Full literature synthesis is scheduled per [RESEARCH_ROADMAP.md](../../RESEARCH_ROADMAP.md#phase-1--foundations-2026-q2q3).

## Status

This document is a placeholder-free scaffold, not yet populated with a completed review — consistent with this repository's standard of not asserting a synthesis that hasn't been done. It exists now so that structure, citation format, and scope are set before the review work begins, and so contributors know exactly where to add sources as they're reviewed.
