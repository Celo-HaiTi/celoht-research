# Climate Research

**Summary:** Research on Haiti's climate context and its relevance to CeloHT's environmental and community-development work.
**Purpose:** Ground the Reforestation pillar in accurate, current climate science for the specific region CeloHT operates in.
**Scope:** Regional climate trends, deforestation drivers, climate-economic vulnerability.
**Audience:** Reforestation Working Group, researchers, environmental partners.

## Overview

Reforestation only makes sense against a clear understanding of the climate pressures it's responding to. This area studies that context directly, distinct from `research/reforestation/`, which focuses on CeloHT's own planting program execution.

## Research Questions

1. What are the primary drivers of deforestation in CeloHT's operating region specifically (as opposed to Haiti-wide generalizations)?
2. How does climate vulnerability intersect with financial vulnerability for the communities CeloHT serves?
3. What species and planting approaches are best supported by current regional climate projections, not just historical norms?

## Objectives

Ensure CeloHT's reforestation design decisions (see `research/reforestation/`) rest on current regional climate evidence.

## Current Knowledge

Foundational stage.

## Data Sources

Published climate and environmental research on Haiti; regional agricultural/environmental partner input (see the main documentation repository's `REFORESTATION.md#scientific-methodology--partnerships`).

## Methodology

Primarily literature review and partner consultation — see [../../METHODOLOGY.md](../../METHODOLOGY.md).

## Limitations

Fine-grained, hyper-local climate data for specific pilot sites may be limited; this is disclosed explicitly rather than substituted with national-level generalizations presented as site-specific.

## Future Work

Site-specific climate risk assessment for current and planned reforestation locations.
