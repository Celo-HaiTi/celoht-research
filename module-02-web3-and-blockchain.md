# Module 2: Introduction to Web3 & Blockchain

*Part of the CeloHT Education curriculum — see [../EDUCATION.md](../EDUCATION.md).*
*Previous: [Module 1 →](./module-01-financial-literacy-foundations.md)*

## Audience
Individuals who have completed Module 1, or developers/entrepreneurs seeking a plain-language grounding before technical material.

## Duration
Approx. 60 minutes.

## Learning Objectives

1. Explain what a blockchain is, in plain language, without jargon
2. Understand why CeloHT uses a blockchain (Celo) rather than a traditional database
3. Correctly state that CeloHT has no token — see [../NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md](../NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md)

## Lesson 2.1 — What Is a Blockchain, Really?

**Analogy used in teaching materials:** A blockchain is like a shared notebook that many people keep an identical copy of. When someone writes a new entry (a transaction), everyone's copy updates the same way, and old entries can't be secretly erased or changed. No single person controls the notebook.

**Why this matters for CeloHT:** it means CeloHT's Agent Network transactions (see [../AGENT_NETWORK.md](../AGENT_NETWORK.md)) can be independently verified — nobody, including CeloHT itself, can quietly alter the record.

## Lesson 2.2 — Why Celo Specifically

**Key points, taught simply:**
- Built to work well on regular smartphones, including lower-end ones
- Transaction costs are small and predictable
- Has a "stable" digital dollar (cUSD) — not something whose value jumps around like some other digital assets

See [../CELO.md](../CELO.md) for the full technical rationale (reference-level, for developers/advanced learners).

## Lesson 2.3 — Clearing Up Common Confusion

This lesson directly and explicitly addresses misconceptions participants likely bring from other contexts:

| Misconception | Correction |
|---|---|
| "This is like buying a cryptocurrency to get rich" | CeloHT has no token to buy. cUSD is used for payments, not speculation — see [../NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md](../NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md) |
| "I need to understand complex technology to use this safely" | You need to understand your wallet and how to verify a transaction — covered in [Module 4](./module-04-wallet-safety.md) |
| "Blockchain = crypto scam" | Acknowledge this fear directly — it's often based on real bad experiences elsewhere. Explain what makes CeloHT structurally different (No-Token Policy, community governance, no promised returns) |

## Lesson 2.4 — Public vs. Private Data on CeloHT

**Key concept:** Not everything is public. Explain the difference: transaction amounts and Agent verification status are publicly verifiable (see [../SMART_CONTRACTS.md](../SMART_CONTRACTS.md#design-principles)); personal identity information is not stored on the blockchain (see [../PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md)).

## Assessment

Participant should be able to explain blockchain using their own analogy (not memorized), and correctly state that CeloHT has no token, in their own words.
