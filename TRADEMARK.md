# Trademark

## What's Trademarked

The "CeloHT" name and logo function as CeloHT's trademark/brand identifiers. This is separate from the Apache 2.0 License covering code and documentation — open-source licensing doesn't automatically grant unrestricted trademark use. See [LICENSE](./LICENSE) and [LEGAL_STATUS.md](./LEGAL_STATUS.md#intellectual-property).

## Permitted Use

- Referencing, linking to, or discussing CeloHT factually (nominative use)
- Using the logo per the Brand repository's `LOGO_GUIDELINES.md` for legitimate community, educational, or press purposes
- Co-branding under a signed agreement — see [PARTNERSHIP_BRANDING.md](https://github.com/Celo-HT/Brand/blob/main/PARTNERSHIP_BRANDING.md) (Brand repository)

## Prohibited Use

- Implying official partnership, endorsement, or affiliation that doesn't exist — including with Celo Foundation or Valora
- Using the CeloHT name or logo on any token, coin, NFT, or investment offering — see [NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md](./NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md)
- Modifying the logo outside the approved variants in the Brand repository's `logos/` directory
- Registering a domain, social handle, or company name designed to be confused with CeloHT

## Enforcement

Trademark misuse — especially anything implying a "CeloHT token" or investment product — is treated as a security priority, not just a legal formality, because of the direct harm it can cause to people who trust the CeloHT name. See [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md#brand-misuse-is-a-security-issue) and [NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md](./NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md#reporting-violations).

## Reporting Misuse

Email security@celoht.com with the URL/location, a description, and any relevant screenshots. See [CONTACT.md](./CONTACT.md).

## Questions About a Specific Use Case

If you're unsure whether your planned use is covered, ask first at partnerships@celoht.com — it's much easier to clarify in advance than to resolve a public misunderstanding after the fact.
