# No Token Policy

**Status:** Active and permanent policy · **Version:** 1.0 · **Last updated:** 2026-08-01

## Policy Statement

CeloHT does not have, has never had, and will never issue a native token. This is a permanent architectural and governance decision, not a temporary phase.

Specifically, CeloHT has:

- **No native token** of any kind (utility, governance, or otherwise)
- **No ICO** (Initial Coin Offering)
- **No presale** or private sale of any asset
- **No staking token** or staking rewards program
- **No speculation model** — nothing in CeloHT is designed, marketed, or intended to be traded for profit
- **No investment promises** — CeloHT never promises, implies, or suggests financial returns to users, agents, or partners

## What CeloHT Uses Instead

CeloHT operates entirely on **existing, independently-issued Celo network assets**:

- **cUSD** (Celo Dollar) — used for payments, education stipends where applicable, and Agent Network transactions
- **CELO** — used exclusively for network gas fees, never held or promoted as an investment asset by CeloHT

CeloHT did not create, does not control, and does not profit from the issuance of cUSD or CELO. These are pre-existing assets on the Celo network that CeloHT uses as payment infrastructure, in the same way a merchant uses an existing national currency.

## Why This Policy Exists

1. **Trust.** Communities CeloHT serves have often been targets of speculative schemes that promised returns and delivered loss. A no-token policy removes that risk structurally, not just rhetorically.
2. **Mission alignment.** CeloHT's mission is financial *inclusion* — access to usable tools — not financial *speculation*. A token would create incentives misaligned with that mission (e.g. pressure to inflate adoption numbers to support token value).
3. **Regulatory clarity.** Avoiding a token significantly simplifies CeloHT's legal and regulatory position across the jurisdictions it operates in — see [LEGAL_STATUS.md](./LEGAL_STATUS.md).
4. **Governance integrity.** Community governance (see [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md)) is based on participation and contribution, not token-weighted voting, which tends to concentrate power with the wealthiest holders.

## What This Means in Practice

- No CeloHT communication, document, or representative may describe CeloHT participation as an "investment" or promise financial "returns"
- No CeloHT material may reference a "CeloHT token," "$CELOHT," or similar — any such reference found anywhere is unauthorized and should be reported per [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md)
- Agents in the [Agent Network](./AGENT_NETWORK.md) are compensated through a transparent, published fee/incentive structure denominated in cUSD for services rendered — not through token rewards or appreciation
- Grant or donor funding (see [PARTNERS.md](./PARTNERS.md)) is denominated in fiat or cUSD, never structured as a token allocation

## Reporting Violations

If you encounter any material — a website, social account, or app — claiming to offer a "CeloHT token," presale, or investment opportunity, this is **fraudulent and not affiliated with CeloHT**. Report immediately to contact@celoht.com and see [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md#brand-misuse-is-a-security-issue).

## Policy Amendment Process

Because this policy is foundational to community trust, changing it requires:

1. A public RFC (request for comment) open for a minimum of 60 days
2. Supermajority approval from the Maintainer Council (see [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md))
3. Public community comment period with documented responses to all substantive objections

In practice, this policy is intended to never be reversed — the process above exists to make that structurally difficult, not to suggest reversal is likely or planned.
