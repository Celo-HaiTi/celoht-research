# Reforestation

> **Current status:** As of this writing, the Reforestation program is in its design and pilot phase — the operational procedures below (in [`reforestation/`](./reforestation/)) describe the model being stood up for the first pilot, targeted for [Phase 2 of the roadmap](./ROADMAP.md#phase-2--validation-2026-q4--2027-q1), not an already-mature, multi-region operation. This document and its companion manuals describe the intended, designed process — treat status claims about "current" activity as pilot-stage unless [METRICS.md](./METRICS.md) or a dated community update says otherwise.

## Purpose

Reforestation ensures CeloHT's economic development work does not come at environmental cost — and actively reinvests in the communities it serves. This is a permanent, resourced pillar, not an offset or PR gesture.

## Program Components

### Tree Planting
Community-led planting programs using native and regionally appropriate species, selected in consultation with local agricultural and environmental partners. **Full planting methodology:** [reforestation/planting-methodology.md](./reforestation/planting-methodology.md)

### Community Nurseries
Local nurseries, established and maintained by community members (often coordinated through the Agent Network's local presence), providing saplings for planting programs and building long-term local capacity rather than one-time external planting events. **Full nursery operations manual:** [reforestation/nursery-operations.md](./reforestation/nursery-operations.md)

### Climate Education
Environmental education modules integrated into the broader Education pillar — see [EDUCATION.md](./EDUCATION.md) — covering local climate impact, soil health, and the community benefits of reforestation.

### Volunteer Management
Structured volunteer program for community members and diaspora supporters, with defined roles (planting, nursery maintenance, monitoring) and recognition system. **Full volunteer manual:** [reforestation/volunteer-management.md](./reforestation/volunteer-management.md)

## Impact Measurement

**Full monitoring and survival-tracking manual:** [reforestation/monitoring-and-survival.md](./reforestation/monitoring-and-survival.md)

### Methodology
- Each planting event is logged with: location (GPS-tagged), species, quantity, date, and responsible community group
- Survival rate monitoring at 6-month and 18-month intervals — a tree "counted" in CeloHT's public metrics is a **surviving** tree, not merely a planted one
- Community-reported and independently spot-verified monitoring, to guard against overreporting

### GIS Integration
Planting locations are logged with GPS coordinates and aggregated into a public map, allowing communities and partners to see exactly where reforestation activity has occurred.

### Satellite Monitoring Concepts
For larger planting areas, CeloHT's roadmap includes integrating satellite-based vegetation monitoring (e.g. NDVI analysis) to independently verify canopy growth over time as a supplement to — not replacement for — community ground reporting. See [ROADMAP.md](./ROADMAP.md).

### Carbon Impact
Where scientifically defensible, estimated carbon sequestration is reported using established regional biomass methodologies, always presented as an estimate range rather than a precise, marketable figure — CeloHT does not sell or trade carbon credits as part of this program.

## Community Incentives

Community members involved in nursery maintenance and planting verification receive transparent, published compensation for their time (denominated in cUSD, consistent with [NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md](./NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md)), not speculative or token-based rewards.

## Environmental Reporting

Reforestation outcomes are published as part of CeloHT's overall impact reporting — see [METRICS.md](./METRICS.md) — including: trees planted, verified survival rate, hectares under active monitoring, and active community nurseries.

## Scientific Methodology & Partnerships

Reforestation methodology is developed and periodically reviewed in partnership with environmental and agricultural organizations (see [PARTNERS.md](./PARTNERS.md)) to ensure species selection, planting density, and monitoring practices reflect current best practice rather than assumption.

## Long-Term Sustainability

Reforestation success is measured over years, not planting events. The program is explicitly designed around community ownership — nurseries and monitoring are locally run — so impact continues independent of any single funding cycle.
