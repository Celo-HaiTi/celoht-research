# Success Stories

## Purpose & Standard

Success stories are narrative, human-centered complements to [CASE_STUDIES.md](./CASE_STUDIES.md) — where case studies emphasize methodology and metrics, success stories emphasize the lived experience of a community member, agent, or partner, while holding to the same honesty standard: real, attributed (with consent), and not exaggerated.

## What Qualifies

A success story should be tied to a verifiable program activity and, where it references outcomes, should use figures consistent with [METRICS.md](./METRICS.md) rather than informal estimates.

## Story Structure

```markdown
# [Person/Community Name]'s Story

## Before
[Context — what challenge or gap existed]

## What Changed
[Which CeloHT pillar(s) were involved, what specifically happened]

## In Their Words
[Direct quote, with consent, ideally in the storyteller's original language with translation]

## Outcome
[What's different now — grounded, not overstated]
```

## Editorial Standards

- Never imply a financial return or investment outcome, consistent with [../NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md](./NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md) — a success story is about access, confidence, or opportunity, never about money "growing"
- Always obtain and document consent before publishing a community member's story, photo, or quote
- Avoid presenting a single story as representative of typical outcomes without the supporting aggregate data from [METRICS.md](./METRICS.md)

## Publication

Success stories are published on the website and cross-posted to social channels per the Brand repository's `SOCIAL_MEDIA_GUIDE.md`. This document defines the standard; individual published stories are added as they're collected and verified — see the incremental-content note in [AUTHORS.md](./AUTHORS.md#note-on-this-list).
