# Maintainers

## Current Structure

CeloHT's maintainer structure follows [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md): a Foundation Director, a Maintainer Council, and Community Contributors who can be nominated to the Council through sustained, quality contribution.

## Foundation Director

**Johnny Dubic** — see [FOUNDER.md](./FOUNDER.md).

## Maintainer Council

Council membership by repository is maintained in each repository's own settings and, where relevant, a repository-specific `MAINTAINERS.md` — this file describes the *model*, since a hardcoded list here would drift out of date faster than it could be kept accurate. See [AUTHORS.md](./AUTHORS.md) for contributor recognition, which is updated as contributions are merged.

## What Maintainers Do

- Review and merge pull requests within their area (see [WORKING_GROUPS.md](./WORKING_GROUPS.md))
- Triage issues (see [templates/issue-triage-response.md](./templates/issue-triage-response.md) for the standard response process)
- Participate in governance decisions per [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md#decision-making)
- Uphold [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) in all community interactions

## Becoming a Maintainer

There's no fixed application form — Council nomination follows sustained, high-quality contribution over time. In practice: start with issues tagged `good-first-issue`, keep contributing consistently, and engage constructively in reviews and discussions. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md) and [FIRST_CONTRIBUTION.md](./FIRST_CONTRIBUTION.md).

## Stepping Down

Maintainers can step back from active review responsibilities at any time — CeloHT is volunteer-heavy, and burnout helps no one. Stepping down doesn't erase past contribution recognition in [AUTHORS.md](./AUTHORS.md).
