# Executive Summary

CeloHT is a community-driven initiative built on the Celo blockchain with a mission to expand financial inclusion, environmental restoration, and Web3 education.

Since its founding in 2022, CeloHT has focused on delivering measurable community impact before investing in large-scale media coverage. Most early activities were carried out directly with communities and coordinated through local volunteers and community agents.

This report summarizes operational impact reported between 2022 and 2026. Where complete historical records are unavailable, values are clearly identified as estimates based on internal reporting from community coordinators and agent leaders.

# Pillar One — Education

CeloHT provides practical education focused on:

- Financial literacy
- Blockchain fundamentals
- Celo ecosystem
- cUSD
- Valora Wallet
- Responsible Web3 adoption

Between 2022 and 2026, CeloHT estimates that more than **3,095 people** participated in educational activities through community workshops, online sessions, social media, and peer-to-peer learning.

Communities reached include participants from:

- Haiti
- Dominican Republic
- United States
- Canada
- Brazil
- Chile
- Mexico

# Data Collection Methodology

The information contained in this report was compiled using internal operational records collected from:

- Community agent reports
- Local coordinators
- Volunteer activity logs
- Educational program records
- Community leadership reports

During the organization's early years, the priority was direct community impact rather than systematic media documentation. Consequently, some historical activities were not consistently supported by photographs or video evidence.

Future reporting will progressively integrate:

- GPS location verification
- Photo documentation
- Video documentation
- Public impact dashboards
- Blockchain analytics where applicable
