# CeloHT Voice & Tone

## Voice (constant across all content)

CeloHT's voice is:

- **Clear, not clever** — we write for someone accessing financial tools for the first time, not for crypto insiders. If a sentence needs a glossary, rewrite it.
- **Warm, not corporate** — we're a community initiative, not a company selling a product.
- **Confident, not hyped** — we describe real impact and real tools. We never promise returns, gains, or guaranteed outcomes.
- **Respectful of language** — Haitian Creole is treated as a primary language, not a translation afterthought. Content should be written natively in Creole where possible, not machine-translated from English.

## Tone (adapts by context)

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Educational content | Patient, encouraging, step-by-step |
| Community announcements | Warm, direct, transparent |
| Technical documentation | Precise, neutral, thorough |
| Social media | Friendly, concise, human |
| Press / official statements | Measured, factual, credible |

## Words We Use

- "financial inclusion," "community-governed," "open source," "education," "local support"
- "tools," "access," "learn," "grow," "together"

## Words We Avoid

- "investment," "returns," "profit," "get rich," "guaranteed"
- "official Celo product" or any phrasing implying Celo Foundation/Valora affiliation
- Jargon-heavy crypto terms without a plain-language explanation alongside them

## Example

**On-brand:**
> "CeloHT te kreye pou ede kominote yo aprann ak itilize zouti finansye dijital yo, ansanm." *(CeloHT was created to help communities learn and use digital financial tools, together.)*

**Off-brand:**
> "Join CeloHT and start earning returns on the Celo blockchain today!" — implies investment returns and overstates affiliation; never use this framing.

## Non-Affiliation Language (required)

Always available in three languages for consistency:

- **English:** "CeloHT is an independent, community-governed initiative. It is not officially affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Celo Foundation or Valora."
- **Français:** "CeloHT est une initiative indépendante, gérée par la communauté. Elle n'est pas officiellement affiliée à, approuvée par, ou exploitée par Celo Foundation ou Valora."
- **Kreyòl Ayisyen:** "CeloHT se yon inisyativ endepandan, kominote a jere li. Li pa afilye ofisyèlman ak, li pa apwouve pa, e li pa opere pa Celo Foundation oswa Valora."
