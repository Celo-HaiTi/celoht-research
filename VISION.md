# CeloHT Vision

## The Vision

**An inclusive, sustainable digital future where every community — starting in Haiti — has real access to education, financial tools, and opportunity, regardless of income, location, or prior technical experience.**

## Why This Matters

Millions of people across Haiti lack access to formal banking, stable financial education, or reliable digital payment tools — while at the same time carrying smartphones capable of running the infrastructure that could solve this. The gap isn't technology. It's access, trust, and education delivered in the right language, by the right people, in the right way.

CeloHT exists to close that gap — not by dropping technology into communities from the outside, but by building education, local human support (the Agent Network), and environmental stewardship (Reforestation) into the same initiative, so financial inclusion grows alongside community trust and long-term sustainability.

## What Success Looks Like

- Haitian Creole-speaking communities can learn financial literacy and digital payments in their own language, from trusted local sources
- A trained, community-rooted Agent Network gives people a human point of contact for digital financial tools — not just an app with no support
- Financial inclusion efforts are paired with environmental impact (Reforestation), so growth doesn't come at the community's long-term expense
- CeloHT's governance model, documentation, and code are transparent and open enough that any community, anywhere, could adapt this model for their own context

## Ten-Year Horizon

CeloHT aims to become a reference model — proof that community-governed, open-source, education-first approaches to financial inclusion can outperform top-down, extractive alternatives, and that this model can be responsibly replicated beyond Haiti by communities who choose to adopt it.
