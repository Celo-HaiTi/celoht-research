# cUSD (Celo Dollar)

## What cUSD Is

cUSD is a stable-value digital asset on the Celo network, designed to track the value of the US dollar. cUSD is issued independently by the Celo protocol — **CeloHT does not issue, control, or profit from cUSD's creation.** CeloHT uses cUSD purely as a payment medium.

## Why CeloHT Uses cUSD (Not CELO) for Payments

CELO's price fluctuates like most crypto assets; cUSD does not, by design. For everyday payments — the core use case for CeloHT's Agent Network and dApp — price stability matters far more to users than potential appreciation. This is also why CeloHT's No-Token Policy (see [NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md](./NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md)) is compatible with real payment utility: cUSD isn't a speculative asset, it's a digital representation of a stable currency value.

## How cUSD Is Used in CeloHT

- **Agent Network transactions** — cash-in converts physical cash to cUSD; cash-out converts cUSD to cash (see [AGENT_NETWORK.md](./AGENT_NETWORK.md))
- **Agent compensation** — published per-transaction fees paid in cUSD (see [AGENT_NETWORK.md](./AGENT_NETWORK.md#revenue-model))
- **Community/reforestation incentive payments** — see [REFORESTATION.md](./REFORESTATION.md#community-incentives)
- **Grant/partner funding disbursement** where applicable — see [PARTNERS.md](./PARTNERS.md)

## What cUSD Is Not, in CeloHT's Context

- Not a CeloHT-issued asset
- Not promoted or described by CeloHT as an investment or appreciating asset
- Not used for gas fees (that's CELO's role — see [CELO.md](./CELO.md))
