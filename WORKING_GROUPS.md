# Working Groups

## Purpose

Working Groups carry out CeloHT's day-to-day direction within their domain, reporting to the Maintainer Council — see [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md#working-groups) for their place in the overall governance structure.

## Active Working Groups

### Education Working Group
**Scope:** Curriculum design, localization, educator training, train-the-trainer programs.
**Key documents owned:** [EDUCATION.md](./EDUCATION.md)

### Agent Network Working Group
**Scope:** Agent lifecycle, training, compliance, regional coordination, fraud prevention.
**Key documents owned:** [AGENT_NETWORK.md](./AGENT_NETWORK.md)

### Reforestation Working Group
**Scope:** Planting programs, community nurseries, environmental monitoring, scientific methodology partnerships.
**Key documents owned:** [REFORESTATION.md](./REFORESTATION.md)

### Engineering Working Group
**Scope:** Smart contracts, dApp, API, infrastructure, security, on-call incident response.
**Key documents owned:** [ARCHITECTURE.md](./ARCHITECTURE.md), [SMART_CONTRACTS.md](./SMART_CONTRACTS.md), [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md), [MONITORING.md](./MONITORING.md)

### Governance & Legal Working Group
**Scope:** Policy, legal status, partnership terms, dispute resolution, RFC process facilitation.
**Key documents owned:** [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md), [LEGAL_STATUS.md](./LEGAL_STATUS.md), [PARTNERS.md](./PARTNERS.md)

## How Working Groups Operate

- Each Working Group has a lead, appointed/rotated by the Maintainer Council
- Working Groups meet on a regular cadence (published internally) and post public summary notes
- Working Groups have delegated authority over standard decisions in their domain; foundational changes still require the full RFC process in [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md#decision-making-process)

## Joining a Working Group

Sustained, quality contribution in a domain (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md)) is the primary path to joining a Working Group. There is no fixed size limit — Working Groups grow as qualified, active contributors emerge.

## Cross-Working-Group Coordination

Initiatives spanning multiple pillars (e.g. a partnership touching both Education and Agent Network) are coordinated jointly, with the Governance & Legal Working Group facilitating alignment where needed.
