# CeloHT Internal Controls Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** August 2026
**Status:** Active

---

## Purpose

This document defines the internal control mechanisms CeloHT uses to prevent financial error, misuse, and unilateral control of funds, and to keep the project prepared for external audit at any time. It operationalizes the internal-controls commitments referenced in `FINANCIAL_TRANSPARENCY.md` Section 6.

## Scope

This policy applies to all financial processes: budgeting, procurement, expense approval, donation handling, and treasury custody, as well as the records generated by each.

## Responsibilities

| Role | Internal Controls Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Treasury Committee | Executes controls in daily treasury operations |
| Finance Working Group | Maintains records, performs reconciliations |
| Governance Council | Oversees control design and reviews control effectiveness annually |

## Review Schedule

Reviewed annually per `GOVERNANCE.md` Section 21, and immediately following any identified control failure or discrepancy.

---

## 1. Segregation of Duties

No single individual controls an entire financial transaction end-to-end. CeloHT separates the following functions:

| Function | Typical Responsible Party |
|---|---|
| Requesting a expense or procurement | Working Group Lead |
| Approving the request | Treasury Committee or Governance Council, per `EXPENSE_APPROVAL_POLICY.md` |
| Executing the disbursement | Treasury Committee, under multi-signature control (`TREASURY.md` Section 4) |
| Recording and reconciling the transaction | Finance Working Group |

This separation ensures that the person who identifies a need is not the same person who approves the spending, and the person who approves spending is not the same person who has sole custody of the funds.

## 2. Approval Workflow

All financial transactions follow the approval workflows defined in `TREASURY.md` Section 3, `PROCUREMENT_POLICY.md` Section 4, and `EXPENSE_APPROVAL_POLICY.md` Section 2. Internal controls require that no transaction is executed without documented approval matching the applicable threshold, and that emergency exceptions follow the ratification process in `EXPENSE_APPROVAL_POLICY.md` Section 3.

## 3. Documentation Controls

Every financial transaction is documented with, at minimum:

- The transaction date, amount, and category.
- The requesting individual or Working Group.
- The approving individual(s), with recusals noted per `CONFLICT_OF_INTEREST_FINANCE.md`.
- Supporting evidence (invoice, receipt, grant agreement, or equivalent).

Documentation is created contemporaneously with the transaction, not reconstructed after the fact for reporting purposes.

## 4. Financial Records Retention

- Financial records (approvals, supporting documentation, ledgers, and reports) are retained for a minimum of seven years, consistent with common nonprofit and audit-readiness practice, or longer where required by an applicable legal or grant-agreement obligation.
- Records are stored in a manner that preserves their integrity and accessibility for internal review and external audit.
- Access to financial records is limited to individuals with a legitimate governance, finance, or audit role, consistent with `ARCHITECTURE.md` Section 12.3.

## 5. Audit Readiness

CeloHT structures its internal controls so that, at any point, its financial records could be reviewed by an external auditor without requiring reconstruction of missing documentation. Audit readiness practices include:

- Consistent transaction categorization aligned with `TREASURY.md` Section 6 and `FUND_ALLOCATION_FRAMEWORK.md`.
- Reconciliation between on-chain transaction records and off-chain accounting records, performed regularly by the Finance Working Group.
- Retention of all documentation described in Section 3 and 4 above.
- Cooperation with the independent review process defined in `AUDIT_POLICY.md` once CeloHT's activity reaches the applicable threshold.

As of this document's publication date, CeloHT has not yet undergone an external audit; this is disclosed explicitly in `AUDIT_POLICY.md` rather than implied otherwise.
