# CeloHT Financial Reports

**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** August 2026
**Status:** Active — Templates Published, Historical Reports Pending Publication

---

## Purpose

This document defines the standard templates CeloHT uses for financial reporting, so that every report published — regardless of who prepares it — follows a consistent, comparable format. It does not contain historical financial data; as of this document's publication date, no reporting period has yet closed with published figures, and this is stated explicitly rather than populated with illustrative numbers.

## Scope

This document applies to all recurring CeloHT financial reports: Monthly Reports, Quarterly Reports, Annual Reports, Treasury Summaries, and Budget Summaries.

## Responsibilities

| Role | Reporting Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Finance Working Group | Prepares each report using the templates below |
| Treasury Committee | Verifies treasury figures before publication |
| Governance Council | Reviews and approves the Annual Report prior to publication |

## Review Schedule

Templates are reviewed annually per `GOVERNANCE.md` Section 21, or sooner if a structural gap is identified during report preparation.

---

## 1. Reporting Cadence

| Report | Frequency | Publication Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly Report | Monthly | Within 15 days of month-end |
| Quarterly Report | Quarterly | Within 30 days of quarter-end |
| Annual Report | Annually | Within 60 days of year-end |
| Treasury Summary | Quarterly | Alongside the Quarterly Report |
| Budget Summary | Annually, with quarterly check-ins | Alongside the annual budget approval and each Quarterly Report |

## 7. Publication Standard

All published reports:

- Use the templates above without omitting sections; sections without available data are marked "Not Yet Available" rather than deleted, so that the absence of data is visible rather than silent.
- Are dated and versioned, with prior versions retained in the repository's version history.
- Are cross-checked for consistency with `TREASURY.md`, `FUND_ALLOCATION_FRAMEWORK.md`, and any prior report before publication.
