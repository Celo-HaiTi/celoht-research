# Stablecoins Research

**Summary:** Research on cUSD usage patterns, price stability in practice, and payment behavior.
**Purpose:** Verify cUSD functions as intended for CeloHT's users — stable, usable, trusted.
**Scope:** cUSD transaction patterns, perceived vs. actual stability, comparison to cash and alternative digital payment rails.
**Audience:** Researchers, Engineering, financial-inclusion partners.

## Overview

cUSD's stability is central to CeloHT's payment model (see the main documentation repository's `CUSD.md`). This area studies whether that stability holds up in practice for CeloHT's specific users and use cases, and how people actually perceive and use it.

## Research Questions

1. How does cUSD's real-world price stability compare to user expectations and to physical cash's practical stability (accounting for theft/loss risk)?
2. What transaction patterns emerge — savings behavior, immediate spend-through, remittance use?
3. How well do users distinguish cUSD (stable, for payments) from CELO (volatile, gas-only) after education (see `research/education/`)?

## Objectives

Confirm cUSD is delivering the practical benefit CeloHT's model assumes, and catch early if it isn't.
