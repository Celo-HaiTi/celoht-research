# Reforestation Research

**Summary:** Research on planting methodology, survival rates, and ecological impact of CeloHT's reforestation pilot.
**Purpose:** Ground the Reforestation pillar's public claims in verified ecological data, not planting counts alone.
**Scope:** Species selection, survival methodology, community nursery effectiveness, environmental impact measurement.
**Audience:** Reforestation Working Group, environmental partners, researchers.

## Overview

CeloHT's own operational standard already refuses to report "trees planted" without a paired survival rate (see the main documentation repository's `REFORESTATION.md#impact-measurement`). This research area is where the methodology behind that standard is developed, tested, and improved — and where CeloHT's environmental claims get the same evidentiary scrutiny as its financial-inclusion claims.

## Research Questions

1. What survival rate is actually being achieved at the pilot site(s), and how does it vary by species and planting method?
2. Which factors (site selection, species choice, community nursery quality, monitoring frequency) most affect survival?
3. Is community-based monitoring data reliable compared to independent spot-verification, and by how much do they diverge?
4. What is a scientifically defensible estimate of carbon sequestration for CeloHT's specific planting mix and region, if any claim is to be made at all?

## Objectives

Produce a validated, region-specific planting and monitoring methodology before the program scales beyond the pilot — see [Phase 2 of the program roadmap](https://github.com/Celo-HT/CeloHT/blob/main/ROADMAP.md#phase-2--validation-2026-q4--2027-q1).

## Current Knowledge

Foundational stage. The main documentation repository's `reforestation/` operational manuals describe the *designed* process; this research area exists to validate whether that design is actually producing good survival outcomes once real pilot data exists.

## Data Sources

Planting event logs, 30-day/6-month/18-month monitoring checkpoints (see the main documentation repository's `reforestation/monitoring-and-survival.md`), partner agronomist consultation records.

## Methodology

Primarily ecological field research — see [../../FIELD_RESEARCH.md](../../FIELD_RESEARCH.md) and [../../METHODOLOGY.md](../../METHODOLOGY.md#research-types-used-at-celoht).

## Limitations

Pilot-scale data (a small number of sites, a short time horizon) limits how confidently findings generalize to future, larger plantings — this is stated explicitly in any published finding until multi-site, multi-year data exists.
