# Module 6: Responsible Digital Finance

*Part of the CeloHT Education curriculum — see [../EDUCATION.md](../EDUCATION.md).*
*Previous: [Module 5 →](./module-05-digital-security.md)*

## Audience
General audience, with an entrepreneur-focused variant covered in [Lesson 6.4](#lesson-64-for-small-business--entrepreneurs).

## Duration
Approx. 45 minutes.

## Learning Objectives

1. Understand why CeloHT is not an investment and never promises returns
2. Recognize the difference between using a payment tool and speculating on an asset
3. Apply basic budgeting and cross-border payment concepts using CeloHT tools

## Lesson 6.1 — CeloHT Is a Tool, Not an Investment

Directly reinforces [../NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md](../NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md). Teaching framing: "Using CeloHT is like using a bank card or a money transfer service — it moves value you already have. It does not grow your money by itself, and nobody at CeloHT will ever tell you it will."

## Lesson 6.2 — Recognizing Speculative Pressure

Discussion of how speculative schemes elsewhere in Web3 create pressure ("get in early," "limited time") — and why CeloHT's slow, transparent, education-first governance process (see [../GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md)) is deliberately the opposite of that pattern.

## Lesson 6.3 — Using cUSD Responsibly

Practical guidance: cUSD is well suited for spending, saving for a specific near-term goal, and sending/receiving payments — not for treating as a speculative holding, since its whole design purpose is stability, not growth.

## Lesson 6.4 — For Small Business & Entrepreneurs

Extension content for entrepreneur audiences (see [../EDUCATION.md](../EDUCATION.md#audiences--programs)):
- Basic digital bookkeeping using transaction history as a record
- Using CeloHT/Agent Network for supplier payments and customer receipts
- Cross-border payment use case: receiving payment from a diaspora customer or partner via cUSD, with predictable, low fees (see [../CELO.md](../CELO.md#cross-border-payments))

## Lesson 6.5 — Decentralized Finance (DeFi) Basics — Conceptual Only

A brief, deliberately conceptual-only overview of what DeFi is in the broader ecosystem, framed entirely around understanding risk rather than encouraging participation. CeloHT does not offer DeFi products and does not encourage learners to seek them out as a next step from this curriculum.

## Assessment

Participant can explain, in their own words, why CeloHT is not an investment, and can describe one responsible and one risky way to use a stable digital asset like cUSD.
