# Foundation

## What "Foundation" Means for CeloHT

CeloHT does not currently operate as a registered foundation entity in the way some blockchain ecosystems do (e.g. a Swiss or Cayman foundation issuing a token). This document describes CeloHT's foundational structure as it exists: a community-governed open-source initiative, and the principles that would govern any future formalization of that structure.

## Current Structure

CeloHT operates today as an open-source project under community governance — see [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md) — with its legal and organizational status disclosed and kept current at [celoht.com/legal](https://celoht.com) and summarized in [LEGAL_STATUS.md](./LEGAL_STATUS.md).

## Founding Principles

These principles are treated as foundational — changing them requires the elevated governance process described in [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md#decision-making-process):

1. **No token, ever**, as a structural commitment — see [NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md](./NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md)
2. **Community governance**, not founder or investor control — see [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md)
3. **Three permanent pillars** — Education, Agent Network, Reforestation — always funded and developed together, never selectively
4. **Radical transparency** — funding, decisions, and metrics are public by default
5. **Non-affiliation clarity** — Celo and Valora relationships are always disclosed as infrastructure choices, never implied partnerships

## Should CeloHT Formalize as a Legal Foundation

If CeloHT's community decides to formalize into a registered nonprofit or foundation entity, that decision would go through the RFC process in [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md), and would be bound by these constraints:

- The entity structure must not create a mechanism for token issuance
- Governance control must remain with the community structure described in [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md), not transfer to a small board with unilateral authority
- Funding transparency requirements in [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md#funding-governance) must carry over unchanged
