# CeloHT Financial Transparency Framework

**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** August 2026
**Status:** Active

---

## Purpose

This document establishes CeloHT's core financial transparency principles and commitments. It exists to give donors, grant reviewers, ecosystem partners, auditors, and community members a clear, verifiable understanding of how CeloHT manages, reports on, and safeguards financial resources entrusted to it.

This document is the foundation of a broader Financial Governance suite, which includes `TREASURY.md`, `DONATION_POLICY.md`, `PROCUREMENT_POLICY.md`, `EXPENSE_APPROVAL_POLICY.md`, `FINANCIAL_REPORTS.md`, `RESERVE_POLICY.md`, `CONFLICT_OF_INTEREST_FINANCE.md`, `INTERNAL_CONTROLS.md`, `AUDIT_POLICY.md`, `FUND_ALLOCATION_FRAMEWORK.md`, and `FINANCIAL_FAQ.md`. All of these documents operate within, and must remain consistent with, `GOVERNANCE.md`, `LEGAL_STATUS.md`, `FUNDING_POLICY.md`, `NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md`, and `TEAM.md`.

## Scope

This framework applies to all funds received, held, or disbursed in CeloHT's name, regardless of source (grants, donations, programmatic revenue) or form (fiat, cUSD, or any other asset), and to every individual with financial decision-making authority under `GOVERNANCE.md`, including the Founder, the Governance Council, the Treasury Committee, and Working Group Leads with budget responsibility.

## Responsibilities

| Role | Financial Transparency Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Governance Council | Approves budgets and treasury policy; ultimate oversight body |
| Treasury Committee | Executes day-to-day treasury operations under this framework |
| Finance Working Group | Prepares reports, maintains records, supports audit readiness |
| Founder | Subject to the same disclosure and approval rules as any other role; holds no unilateral treasury authority |
| Community members | May request clarification on published reports through official channels |

## Review Schedule

This framework is reviewed at minimum annually, as part of the Annual Governance Review described in `GOVERNANCE.md` Section 21, and whenever CeloHT's institutional or legal status materially changes per `LEGAL_STATUS.md` Section 20.

## 1. Financial Transparency Principles

- **Public by default.** Financial information is published unless a specific, documented reason (such as donor privacy or personal-data protection) requires withholding it.
- **Accurate over convenient.** CeloHT publishes real figures, including unfavorable ones (deficits, delays, unmet targets), rather than presenting only favorable data.
- **No fabricated figures.** CeloHT does not publish placeholder, illustrative, or projected numbers as if they were actuals. Where real data does not yet exist, reports state "Not Yet Available," "Pending Publication," or "To Be Announced (TBA)."
- **Consistency across documents.** Figures reported in one CeloHT document (e.g., `TREASURY.md`) must reconcile with figures reported elsewhere (e.g., `FINANCIAL_REPORTS.md`); discrepancies are corrected and disclosed, not quietly adjusted.

## 2. Accountability

Financial accountability at CeloHT is structural, not aspirational:

- No individual — including the Founder — may unilaterally authorize treasury disbursements above the thresholds defined in `TREASURY.md` and `EXPENSE_APPROVAL_POLICY.md`.
- Every disbursement above the minimum documentation threshold is tied to a named approver, a documented purpose, and a spending category, per `INTERNAL_CONTROLS.md`.
- The Governance Council holds ultimate financial oversight authority and can request a review of any transaction at any time.

## 3. Public Reporting Policy

CeloHT commits to a fixed, predictable reporting cadence, detailed in full in `FINANCIAL_REPORTS.md`:

| Report | Frequency | Publication Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly Report | Monthly | Within 15 days of month-end |
| Quarterly Report | Quarterly | Within 30 days of quarter-end |
| Annual Report | Annually | Within 60 days of year-end |
| Treasury Summary | Quarterly | Alongside the Quarterly Report |

Reports are published in CeloHT's documentation hub and, where applicable, summarized in Haitian Creole and English, consistent with `GOVERNANCE.md` Section 14.2.

## 4. Community Oversight

- Governance Council meeting notes concerning financial matters are published per `GOVERNANCE.md` Section 18.2.
- Community members may raise questions about published financial reports through official CeloHT channels; substantive questions are logged and addressed in the next applicable report or meeting notes.
- Material financial decisions (budget approval, large disbursements, grant acceptance) follow the Proposal Lifecycle in `GOVERNANCE.md` Section 6, which includes a public comment period.

## 5. Disclosure Commitments

CeloHT discloses:

- All treasury inflows and outflows above the documentation threshold defined in `INTERNAL_CONTROLS.md`.
- All grants accepted, including source and general purpose, subject to any donor confidentiality terms disclosed per `DONATION_POLICY.md`.
- Any material financial risk or shortfall identified during a reporting period.
- Any change to this framework or related financial policies, with a dated changelog entry.

Where a specific figure or fact is not yet available — for example, because no external audit has yet occurred — CeloHT states this explicitly rather than omitting the topic.

## 6. Internal Controls

Internal controls (detailed fully in `INTERNAL_CONTROLS.md`) exist to prevent error, misuse, and unilateral control of funds. Core mechanisms include segregation of duties between proposal, approval, and execution of a transaction; multi-person approval for disbursements above defined thresholds; and periodic reconciliation between on-chain records and off-chain accounting.

## 7. Record Keeping

CeloHT maintains records of all financial transactions, approvals, and supporting documentation (invoices, grant agreements, expense receipts) in a format suitable for internal review and, where applicable, external audit. Record retention practices are detailed in `INTERNAL_CONTROLS.md` Section 4.

## 8. Transparency Roadmap

CeloHT's financial transparency practices are expected to formalize further as the project matures:

| Stage | Current Status | Planned Development |
|---|---|---|
| Public reporting cadence | Framework defined in this document | Reports published as financial activity occurs |
| Independent financial review | Not Yet Available | Planned once annual treasury flow exceeds the threshold defined in `AUDIT_POLICY.md` |
| Formal accounting system | Pending Publication | To be documented in `INTERNAL_CONTROLS.md` as adopted |
| Institutional incorporation | Not incorporated (see `LEGAL_STATUS.md` Section 3) | Evaluated per `LEGAL_STATUS.md` Section 20 |

This roadmap will be updated as each stage is reached, with dated changelog entries reflecting actual progress rather than anticipated progress.
