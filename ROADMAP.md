# Roadmap

This is the authoritative roadmap for the CeloHT ecosystem, covering the three permanent pillars — Education, Agent Network, Reforestation — and the strategic infrastructure that supports them.

> **How to read this roadmap.** Dates describe target windows, not guarantees. Each phase depends on the one before it being validated — we'd rather move a milestone than hit a date with a half-working program. Progress is reported publicly through monthly community updates and reflected in [CHANGELOG.md](./CHANGELOG.md).

## Guiding Principles

Every item on this roadmap is built on the same non-negotiable facts about what CeloHT is and isn't:

- CeloHT is a **community-driven, open-source initiative**.
- CeloHT is **not** a blockchain, has **no** native token, issues **no** NFTs, and provides **no** investment products.
- CeloHT is **built on the Celo ecosystem**, using **CELO** for network transactions where appropriate and promoting **cUSD** for accessible digital payments.
- CeloHT develops applications **compatible with Valora** — CeloHT does not own, manage, or operate Valora.

Any roadmap item that would conflict with these principles doesn't belong on this roadmap, however attractive it looks. See [NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md](./NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md) and [LEGAL_STATUS.md](./LEGAL_STATUS.md).

## Phase 1 — Foundation (2026, Q2–Q3)

**Goal:** establish a credible, transparent base — documentation, first agents, first partners.

| Milestone | Pillar | Status | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Publish the official flagship repository | Governance | ✅ Done | 2026-Q3 |
| Core financial & Web3 training material in Haitian Creole | Education | ✅ Done | 2026-Q3 |
| Recruit and train the first cohort of community agents (Léogâne) | Agent Network | 🔄 In progress | 2026-Q3 |

**Success metrics:** stable, repeatable transaction volume through agents week over week · agent 90-day retention above 70% · first verified batch of trees planted, photo/geo-tracked · dApp alpha tested by at least 20 real users with structured feedback.

## Beyond Phase 4 (2029–2030 Horizon)

Two longer-range ambitions sit beyond this roadmap's current Phase 4 scope — named in CeloHT's community materials, not yet broken into concrete, dated milestones the way Phases 1–4 are:

- **International Digital Inclusion Mission** — extending CeloHT's model to communities beyond Haiti, consistent with the Caribbean-expansion ambition in [WHITEPAPER.md](./WHITEPAPER.md#future-vision).
- **Full Environmental Regeneration Program** — a mature, large-scale successor to the Reforestation pilot, once its pilot-phase model (see [REFORESTATION.md](./REFORESTATION.md)) has been validated and scaled through Phases 2–4.

Both will be broken into a proper phase structure once Phase 3 validates what's actually working — committing to detailed 2029–2030 milestones today would be a guess dressed up as a plan.

## Dependencies Between Phases

Each phase depends on the one before it being validated. Geographic expansion deliberately waits until the operational model is proven in a single pilot area, and deep smart-contract integration into the dApp deliberately waits until the basic education-and-wallet experience is solid — see [SMART_CONTRACTS.md](./SMART_CONTRACTS.md#path-to-production).

## How Progress Is Tracked

✅ **Done** — shipped and verifiable today · 🔄 **In progress** — actively being worked on · ⏳ **Planned** — scoped, not yet started.

Progress is reviewed as part of the governance process (see [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md)) and reflected in [CHANGELOG.md](./CHANGELOG.md) as milestones complete.
