# Research Roadmap

**Summary:** What CeloHT Research is studying now, and what's planned next.
**Purpose:** Public accountability for research priorities, matching the transparency standard of CeloHT's program roadmap.
**Scope:** All research areas.
**Audience:** Researchers, partners, funders, community.

> This roadmap tracks alongside — but is distinct from — the program roadmap in the main documentation repository's `ROADMAP.md`. Program milestones describe what CeloHT is *building*; this roadmap describes what CeloHT is *studying*, including studying whether those program milestones actually worked.

## How Priorities Are Set

Research priorities are set to answer the questions CeloHT most needs answered to make good program decisions — not to produce the most publishable or flattering findings. Priorities are reviewed each program phase transition and can be reprioritized via the standard proposal process in [RESEARCH_GUIDELINES.md](./RESEARCH_GUIDELINES.md).
