# Case Studies

> **The full standard and process now live in [celoht-research](https://github.com/Celo-HT/celoht-research).** Rigorous, peer-reviewed case studies are published there under [`PUBLICATION_POLICY.md`](https://github.com/Celo-HT/celoht-research/blob/main/PUBLICATION_POLICY.md#publication-types) and live in [`case-studies/`](https://github.com/Celo-HT/celoht-research/tree/main/case-studies). This page is a short pointer, not a duplicate of that standard.

## How This Differs From Success Stories

[SUCCESS_STORIES.md](./SUCCESS_STORIES.md) covers short, narrative, human-centered stories for the website and blog — not held to a peer-review bar, but still required to be honest and consented. A **case study**, by contrast, goes through celoht-research's full [peer review](https://github.com/Celo-HT/celoht-research/blob/main/PEER_REVIEW.md) process and is expected to hold up to the same scrutiny as any other published research finding. If you're not sure which one your material fits, it's probably a success story unless it includes a documented method and data behind its claims.
