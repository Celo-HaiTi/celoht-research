## What Stays Here

This repository (`celoht-docs`) keeps the **methodology CeloHT's own programs are built on** — the specific, operational standards a program follows day to day:

- Impact measurement methodology: [METRICS.md](./METRICS.md#methodology-principles)
- Reforestation scientific methodology: [REFORESTATION.md](./REFORESTATION.md#scientific-methodology--partnerships)
- Governance decision-making process: [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md#decision-making)

`celoht-research` is where those methodologies get independently tested, questioned, and published as formal findings — the evidence base, not the operating manual. See [celoht-research's README](https://github.com/Celo-HT/celoht-research#relationship-to-other-celoht-repositories) for the same distinction from the other side.

## Partnering on Research

Universities and research institutions proposing a formal study should go directly to [celoht-research/CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/Celo-HT/celoht-research/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md). For a broader partnership relationship (not research-specific), see [PARTNERS.md](./PARTNERS.md#how-to-propose-a-partnership).
