# Ambassador Program

## Purpose

Ambassadors represent CeloHT in their region or professional community — extending outreach beyond what core Working Groups can do directly, while staying accountable to CeloHT's standards.

## Ambassador Types

| Type | Focus |
|---|---|
| Regional Ambassador | Represents CeloHT within a specific geographic community; often works closely with local Agent Network coordination (see [AGENT_NETWORK.md](./AGENT_NETWORK.md#regional-coordinators)) |
| Developer Ambassador | Represents CeloHT within developer communities, conferences, hackathons |
| Campus Ambassador | Represents CeloHT at a specific university, often paired with a research or education partnership (see [PARTNERS.md](./PARTNERS.md)) |

## What Ambassadors Do

- Deliver or support local education workshops (in coordination with the Education Working Group)
- Represent CeloHT accurately at events — including correctly explaining the No-Token Policy (see [NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md](./NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md))
- Provide local feedback to the relevant Working Group
- Help identify Agent Network candidates or partnership opportunities in their region

## What Ambassadors Are Not

Ambassadors do not have governance voting authority beyond standard community participation, do not have unilateral authority to speak on legal or policy matters (routed to the Governance & Legal Working Group), and are not compensated through any token or equity mechanism — consistent with [NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md](./NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md).

## Becoming an Ambassador

1. Sustained community participation (education delivery, code contribution, or event representation) — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md)
2. Nomination or application reviewed by the relevant Working Group
3. Onboarding: CeloHT mission/policy training (including accurate messaging — see the Brand repository's `VOICE_AND_TONE.md` and `MESSAGING_FRAMEWORK.md`)

## Ambassador Standards

Ambassadors are expected to represent CeloHT consistently with [BRAND_GUIDE.md](https://github.com/Celo-HT/Brand/blob/main/BRAND_GUIDE.md) (Brand repository) and this documentation — never implying token existence, investment returns, or unauthorized Celo/Valora affiliation.
