# Template: Partnership Inquiry Response

*Standard first response to an inbound partnership inquiry — see [PARTNERS.md](../PARTNERS.md#how-to-propose-a-partnership).*

---

Hi [Name],

Thank you for reaching out about a potential partnership with CeloHT.

A quick orientation before we go further: CeloHT is a community-driven, open-source initiative — not a company, and not an investment vehicle. We have no token and don't offer equity or financial returns. If your interest is investment-related, this likely isn't a fit, and we'd rather say that clearly now than after a longer conversation — see [NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md](../NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md).

If your interest is in collaborating on our Education, Agent Network, or Reforestation work, here's what's helpful from you:

1. A short description of your organization and what you're proposing
2. Which pillar(s) this connects to — [EDUCATION.md](../EDUCATION.md), [AGENT_NETWORK.md](../AGENT_NETWORK.md), or [REFORESTATION.md](../REFORESTATION.md)
3. Whether funding is involved, and if so, roughly what scope

We'll route this to the relevant area of our Maintainer Council for review — see [GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md#how-the-maintainer-council-organizes-its-work). Initial response time is typically within two weeks.

In the meantime, [WHITEPAPER.md](../WHITEPAPER.md) and [BUSINESS_MODEL.md](../BUSINESS_MODEL.md) give a fuller picture of how we operate.

Best,
CeloHT

---

## Internal Notes for Whoever Sends This

- Do not promise specific outcomes or timelines beyond "initial response in two weeks"
- If the inquiry is clearly investment-seeking, this template's second paragraph should end the conversation politely rather than escalating it
- Log the inquiry per [PARTNERS.md](../PARTNERS.md#partnership-terms) tracking process
