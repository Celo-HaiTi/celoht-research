# Sustainability Research

**Summary:** Research on long-term organizational and environmental sustainability of the CeloHT model.
**Purpose:** Test whether CeloHT's sustainability plan (grant diversification, fee transition, community-owned reforestation) actually holds up over years, not just in design.
**Scope:** Organizational funding resilience, governance continuity, long-horizon environmental outcomes.
**Audience:** Maintainer Council, funders, researchers.

## Overview

The main documentation repository's `SUSTAINABILITY.md` describes CeloHT's intended approach to lasting past any single funding cycle or individual's involvement. This area studies whether that intention is actually being realized as the organization matures.

## Research Questions

1. Is CeloHT's funding actually diversifying over time, or remaining concentrated in a small number of sources?
2. Does the governance model show signs of the founder-dependency it's explicitly designed to avoid (see `GOVERNANCE.md#roadmap-toward-decentralization`), or is that transition on track?
3. Do reforestation sites remain viable years after planting, once initial funding/attention moves elsewhere, validating the community-ownership model?

## Objectives

Provide an honest, periodic check on CeloHT's own long-term viability — the kind of scrutiny a serious funder or partner would want, done by CeloHT itself first.

## Current Knowledge

Foundational stage — meaningful sustainability findings require multi-year data that doesn't exist yet.

## Data Sources

Funding source records (see `FUNDING_POLICY.md#transparency-reporting`), governance participation data (cross-referenced with `research/governance/`), multi-year reforestation survival data (cross-referenced with `research/reforestation/`).

## Methodology

Longitudinal tracking against explicit sustainability indicators — see [../../METHODOLOGY.md](../../METHODOLOGY.md).

## Limitations

Genuine sustainability findings require years of data; early findings here are necessarily about trajectory and leading indicators, not final conclusions.

## Future Work

Establish the specific leading indicators (funding source concentration ratio, governance participation breadth, multi-year survival rate) to track consistently starting now, so a real trend exists to analyze later.
