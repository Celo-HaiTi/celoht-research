# Reforestation Manual: Volunteer Management

*Detailed operational companion to [../REFORESTATION.md](../REFORESTATION.md#volunteer-management). **Status:** describes the volunteer model designed for the Phase 2 pilot (see [../ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md#phase-2--validation-2026-q4--2027-q1)).*

## Volunteer Roles

| Role | Commitment | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Planting-day volunteer | Single event | Helps plant on a scheduled planting day, under coordinator supervision |
| Nursery support volunteer | Ongoing, flexible hours | Assists a paid nursery lead with watering, weeding, and record-keeping |
| Monitoring volunteer | Periodic (per checkpoint schedule) | Assists with 30-day/6-month/18-month survival checks, see [monitoring-and-survival.md](./monitoring-and-survival.md#monitoring-checkpoints) |
| Diaspora supporter | Remote | Fundraising support, spreading awareness, no on-the-ground role required |

## Distinguishing Volunteers from Compensated Roles

Nursery leads and monitoring coordinators are compensated (see [nursery-operations.md](./nursery-operations.md#compensation)) because of the sustained, skilled nature of that work. Planting-day participation is treated as volunteer community involvement, consistent with how many reforestation programs distinguish between one-off community events and ongoing operational roles — this distinction is applied consistently so volunteers understand what to expect before joining.

## Volunteer Onboarding

1. Sign-up through the local Agent Network coordinator or community outreach channel (see [../AGENT_NETWORK.md](../AGENT_NETWORK.md#regional-coordinators))
2. Brief safety and task orientation on the day (proper planting technique, tool safety, site boundaries)
3. Assignment to a specific task and site coordinator for the day

## Recognition

Volunteers are recognized publicly (with consent) in community updates and, where a specific site's success story is documented, in [../SUCCESS_STORIES.md](../SUCCESS_STORIES.md). Recognition is non-monetary for planting-day volunteers, consistent with [../NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md](../NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md) — no token or speculative reward mechanism is used anywhere in the program, including for volunteer appreciation.

## Volunteer Safety

- Basic tool-safety briefing before any planting-day activity
- Water and shade breaks scheduled for outdoor work, given Haiti's climate
- A designated first-aid-capable coordinator present at every organized planting event

## Scaling Volunteer Coordination

As the pilot moves toward Phase 3 expansion (see [../ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md#phase-3--growth-2027)), volunteer coordination is expected to shift from direct founder/coordinator organization toward Regional Coordinators managing volunteer scheduling locally, mirroring the Agent Network's own regional structure (see [../AGENT_NETWORK.md](../AGENT_NETWORK.md#regional-coordinators)).
