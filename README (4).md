# Web3 Research

**Summary:** Broader research on Web3 adoption, perception, and education effectiveness beyond CeloHT-specific tools.
**Purpose:** Situate CeloHT's approach within — and learn from — the wider landscape of Web3-for-development efforts.
**Scope:** Web3 adoption patterns in comparable markets, perception research, comparative program analysis.
**Audience:** Researchers, program designers.

## Overview

CeloHT is one of many attempts to apply blockchain technology to real-world development problems. This area tracks what's been tried elsewhere, what's worked, what hasn't, and why — so CeloHT doesn't repeat avoidable mistakes.

## Research Questions

1. What distinguishes Web3-for-development projects that achieved lasting adoption from those that didn't?
2. How does public perception of "Web3" or "blockchain" as loaded terms affect program uptake, and does avoiding the terminology (as CeloHT's education deliberately does — see the main documentation repository's `education/module-02-web3-and-blockchain.md`) measurably help?
3. What can be learned from comparable financial-inclusion-via-blockchain programs in other regions?

## Objectives

Keep CeloHT's approach informed by the broader field, not developed in isolation.

## Current Knowledge

Foundational stage.

## Data Sources

Published research and case studies from comparable initiatives; CeloHT's own education outcome data (cross-referenced with `research/education/`).

## Methodology

Primarily comparative and literature-based — see [../../METHODOLOGY.md](../../METHODOLOGY.md).

## Limitations

Comparability across contexts (regulatory, cultural, economic) is imperfect; findings from other regions are treated as informative, not directly transferable.

## Future Work

Comparative case study of 3–5 similar Web3-for-financial-inclusion initiatives.
