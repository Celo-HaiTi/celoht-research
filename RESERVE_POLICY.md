# CeloHT Reserve Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** August 2026
**Status:** Active

---

## Purpose

This document defines the structure and rules governing CeloHT's financial reserves — funds set aside deliberately to protect the project's operational continuity, emergency response capacity, and long-term sustainability, independent of near-term programmatic budgets.

## Scope

This policy applies to all treasury funds designated as a reserve under any of the three categories defined in Section 1, and to every approval required to allocate to, or draw from, a reserve.

## Responsibilities

| Role | Reserve Policy Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Governance Council | Approves reserve targets and any reserve drawdown above Treasury Committee authority |
| Treasury Committee | Monitors reserve levels and executes approved allocations/drawdowns |
| Finance Working Group | Reports reserve status in each Treasury Summary and Annual Report |

## Review Schedule

Reviewed annually per `GOVERNANCE.md` Section 21, and immediately following any reserve drawdown.

---

## 1. Reserve Categories

CeloHT maintains three distinct reserve categories, each with a different purpose:

| Reserve | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Operating Reserve** | Covers planned operational costs during temporary funding gaps between grant cycles or donation inflows |
| **Emergency Reserve** | Funds urgent, unplanned needs — such as a security incident response — under the Emergency Decision process in `GOVERNANCE.md` Section 4.5 |
| **Sustainability Reserve** | Supports CeloHT's multi-year continuity and institutional development goals described in `LEGAL_STATUS.md` Section 20 |

## 2. Operating Reserve

- Target size: a minimum of three months of core operational costs, consistent with `GOVERNANCE.md` Section 8.7.
- Funded from unrestricted donations and general grant income not otherwise designated, per `DONATION_POLICY.md` Section 2.
- Drawdown requires Treasury Committee approval within normal expense-approval thresholds (`EXPENSE_APPROVAL_POLICY.md`), since operating-reserve use is a planned, non-emergency function.

## 3. Emergency Reserve

- Target size is set and periodically reviewed by the Governance Council based on identified risk exposure (see `GOVERNANCE.md` Section 20, Risk Management).
- Released only through the Emergency Decision process defined in `GOVERNANCE.md` Section 4.5, or through a standard Governance Council Absolute Majority vote for a planned (non-emergency) allocation change.
- Any Emergency Reserve drawdown is reported publicly within 72 hours and formally ratified by the Governance Council within 14 days, mirroring the Emergency Decision ratification process.

## 4. Sustainability Reserve

- Intended to accumulate gradually as CeloHT's funding base grows, supporting longer-term goals such as institutional incorporation costs (`LEGAL_STATUS.md` Section 20) or multi-year program continuity.
- Allocations to the Sustainability Reserve are approved as part of the annual budget process (`TREASURY.md` Section 3).
- Drawdown from the Sustainability Reserve requires Governance Council Absolute Majority approval and is treated as a Strategic Decision under `GOVERNANCE.md` Section 4.4.

## 5. Reserve Usage Rules

- Reserves are used only for the purpose defined by their category; using Emergency Reserve funds for routine operating shortfalls, or vice versa, requires an explicit, documented Governance Council decision reclassifying the need.
- Reserve balances and any drawdown are reported in the Treasury Summary and Annual Report templates defined in `FINANCIAL_REPORTS.md`.
- As of this document's publication date, CeloHT's reserve balances are in early formation and are reported as "Not Yet Available" until a reporting period closes with actual figures.
